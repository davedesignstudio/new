Caps–script wordmark.

Three lines, one hierarchy. Do not copy the saw blade for every brand—the text structure is the shared system.

  2. Middle · ScriptElegant connected script (The Hills). Largest visual weight; center small connecting words.
  3. Bottom · TaglineSmall all-caps (RENOVATE · REMODEL · RESTORE). Quiet service line under the lockup.
