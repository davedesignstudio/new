About

A design-build company with two toolboxes

Philhower & O’Krogly specializes in carpentry design and build — and in web design and branding. Same standards: measure twice, ship once, leave the mark looking intentional.

On site

Wood, space, finish

We treat carpentry as design work: how the room feels, how the joint reads, how the install holds up after the client lives in it. Design and build stay together so nothing gets lost between the drawing and the saw.

On screen

Brand and web

We build identities that survive embroidery and asphalt — then websites that sound like the same company. Black field, gold type, diamond blue: the lockup leads; the site follows.
