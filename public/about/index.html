About

Philhower & Okrogly

A construction and design company with two specialties that belong together: carpentry you can lean on, and brands that do not flake.

Philhower & Okrogly exists because clients kept asking for both—someone to design the space and someone to design how the business shows up online. Rather than hand work off mid-project, we built one practice.

Okrogly leads carpentry design and build: measuring, drawing, cutting, fitting, and finishing. Philhower leads web design and branding: identity, type, layout, and sites that feel like the shop work—honest materials, clean joints, nothing fake.

The shared rule is simple: if it would not pass in the shop, it does not ship from the studio.

Principles

How we work

Measure twice

Scope, budget, and constraints get written down before anyone reaches for a saw or a blank artboard.

Show the grain

We prefer materials and interfaces that show how they are made—wood grain, clear hierarchy, readable type.

Handoff clean

Clients leave with drawings, brand files, and care notes—not a mystery box of “final-final-v3.”

Building something real?

Whether it starts as a sketch on a napkin or a half-finished site, we will tell you what it takes to finish it well.
