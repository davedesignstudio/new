Studio

We start at the table

D Philhower Studio is a graphic and website design practice rooted in northern New Jersey, working with independent restaurants in and around Morristown.

Every room is different — so the solution should be too. The studio focuses on color, type, and craft that solve real hospitality needs: a clearer brand, a menu guests can actually read, a window that holds the sidewalk, a site that feels like the same place.

Hospitality is the particular strength. Identity, print, and the website are designed together so the language that lives on paper is the language that lives online.

Clients come from Morristown, Madison, Chester, Mendham, Bernardsville, and nearby towns across Morris County who want a local partner instead of a distant template mill.
