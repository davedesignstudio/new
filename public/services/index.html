Process

Listen, shape, print & build

A brand for a restaurant has to survive heat, hands, and weather. We design for that — not for a deck that never leaves the screen.

01 Listen

We eat at your restaurant. We map the block, the regulars, and the gap between how you cook and how you currently look.

02 Shape

Direction, type, color, and a lockup that can be carved, printed, or vinyl-cut. Two or three tight options — not a pile of moods.

03 Print & build

Menus, windows, packaging, and a responsive site, with files and notes so you can keep growing the brand.

Identity

Wordmarks, seals, and color that hold up on glass, kraft, and a black shirt.

Menus & paper

Dinner paper, lunch cards, and pastry lists that guests can read at dusk.

Windows & print

Storefront marks, matchbooks, cups, and the pieces people take home.

Websites

Custom marketing sites that feel like the room — hours, reservations, and the story.
