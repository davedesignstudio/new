Timber & Type

Theme song of Philhower & Okrogly · Mid-tempo craft anthem · Suggested key: G major · ~118 BPM

[Verse 1] Morning light on a workbench scarred, Pencil lines and a coffee jar. Okrogly sets the square and plane, Philhower sketches the mark again. One door to hang, one site to frame— Same steady hand, same honest grain.

[Chorus] Philhower & Okrogly— Design it true, then build it free. From timber joints to glowing screens, We make the thing you meant to mean. Measure twice, cut once, go live— Timber and type, we make it thrive.

[Verse 2] Sawdust floats like a soft refrain, While brand kits dry on the windowpane. A staircase rises, a homepage sings, Cabinets click and the layout rings. No hollow polish, no cheap veneer— If it don’t hold up, it don’t leave here.

[Chorus] Philhower & Okrogly— Design it true, then build it free. From timber joints to glowing screens, We make the thing you meant to mean. Measure twice, cut once, go live— Timber and type, we make it thrive.

[Bridge] Nail by nail and pixel by pixel, Shop floor loud and the studio whistled. Two crafts talking across one table— A room you walk through, a brand that’s able.

[Final chorus] Philhower & Okrogly— Design it true, then build it free. Call the shop when the vision’s ripe— We’ll bring the hammer and the type. Measure twice, cut once, go live— Timber and type… Philhower & Okrogly.

Performance note: keep the verses dry and spoken-sung over acoustic guitar or muted electric; open the chorus with stacked vocals and a handclap/kick pocket. End on an unresolved wood-block tap that answers a soft UI “click.”
