Brand identity kit

Iron Orchard

Wood-grill supper club mark: iron caps, orchard script, supper tagline—built for dining room and truck alike.

Text system

Built on the caps–script wordmark hierarchy: IRON in caps serif, Orchard in script, and WOOD · GRILL · SUPPER in small caps—so the mark reads as craft and premium at every size.

Challenge

Farm-to-grill restaurants need heritage and heat; soft logos disappear on dark menus and road graphics.

Approach

We used the same caps–script wordmark hierarchy, tuned copper against charcoal, then extended the mark into poster, tee, and van.

Outcome

A coherent supper-club kit owners can use from the window to the wrap—one voice, four applications.

Applications

Logo, poster, tee, van

One lockup carried across campaign, merch, and vehicle so the brand feels like a system—not a single file.
