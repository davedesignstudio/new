Text system

Built on the Service The Hills wordmark hierarchy: GILT in caps serif, Tap in script, and COCKTAIL · LOUNGE in small caps—so the mark reads as craft and premium at every size.

Challenge

Approach

Outcome

A premium lockup ready for signage, apparel, and fleet—clear enough to read across a dark room and strong enough to own the curb.

Applications

Logo, poster, tee, van

One lockup carried across campaign, merch, and vehicle so the brand feels like a system—not a single file.
