Foxhollow

A Far Hills inn that needed the maroon-card treatment — a raised gold double-ring seal with a fox, printable from a visiting card to a pickup door.

Place
Far Hills, NJ
Scope
Seal, print, window, site
Palette
Burgundy · gold · cream

Mark

Fox, double ring, burgundy ground

Playfair Display for the wordmark, Allura for “The Inn,” Montserrat for service copy. The seal copies the studio card logic: two gold rings, one animal, one line of serif, one line of script. Rim: Hunt · Table · Fire.

Two-minute Golden exercise: a one-color alternate drawn as a single container plus a single symbol (). If it survives a matchbox, it survives a truck door.

Print

Gold lockup with phone

Seal, wordmark, script, and (908) 555-0160 on matte black — stamped, not printed.

Print

Cloth, cap, truck, cards, sign

The double ring has to hold a burgundy shirt, a cap, a truck door, a hanging inn sign, cards, tees, and a leather apron.

Window

Gold leaf on the door

The fox sits on burgundy millwork so the inn reads before the menu does.
