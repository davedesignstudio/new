North Hollow

A Chester tavern named for the woods behind the building. The identity had to feel older than the renovation — brass, oak, and a seal with a hollow at its center.

Place
Chester, NJ
Scope
Seal, menu, window, site
Palette
Forest · cream · brass

Listen

The room already knew the name

North Hollow sits off Main Street, with a bar that holds the regulars and a dining room that turns for Friday roast. The owners had a good room and a forgettable logo. Guests called it “the hollow” already. The work was to make that nickname printable.

A tavern mark has to work in gold leaf and in ketchup.

Shape

Hollow oak, brass type

Cormorant for the wordmark, Barlow for service copy. The seal is an oak with a dark hollow — readable at two inches on a matchbook and at two feet on the window. Cream paper, forest cloth, brass foil on the dinner menu.
