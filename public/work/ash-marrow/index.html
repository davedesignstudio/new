Ash & Marrow

A Randolph wood-fired room named for what the hearth leaves behind. Charcoal, bone paper, and an ember that has to survive a steel menu.

Randolph, NJ
Scope

Palette

The fire is the room

Ash & Marrow cooks over wood on Sussex Turnpike. The owners had a strong hearth and a forgettable script logo. We sat the pass, listed every cut that sold, and drew crossed marrow bones with a flame at the center — a mark that can brand a board and a window.

If it cannot take smoke, it is not the identity.

Shape

Bones, ash type, ember ink

Oswald for the wordmark, Source Sans 3 for the board. The seal is crossed bones over an ember — stamped on bone paper and cut from steel for the door. The site is charcoal: the fire list, hours, a table near the hearth.
