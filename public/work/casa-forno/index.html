Hospitality

Casa Forno

An Italian trattoria lockup using the studio’s carved-plaster system—serif CASA, script Forno, terracotta arch, and a quiet fire-table tagline.

Text system

Built the same way as Cala and True Olive: CASA in carved caps serif, Forno in gold script, and CUCINA · FIRE · TABLE in small caps. The arch is the oven. The olive is the kitchen. Nothing else is invented on the mark.

Challenge

Italian restaurant brands often lean on flag colors, pizza clipart, or a script that dies on glass. The table needed a lockup that could sit on plaster, a window, and cream dinner paper without being redrawn.

Approach

We kept the studio’s hospitality construction: one lockup, then the street, then the sheet the guest holds. Window gold on dusk stucco. Menu in terracotta and olive on cream. No food photographs on the paper—the kitchen is twenty feet away.

Outcome

A trattoria system ready for signage and dinner service. Studio study, not a live job.

Applications

Logo, window, menu

The same lockup carried to the curb and the table, the way Cala and True Olive leave the studio: mark, street, dinner paper.
