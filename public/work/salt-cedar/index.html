Salt + Cedar

A Morristown oyster bar that needed to smell like the shore without pretending it sits on one. Slate, salt paper, and a shell that holds a cedar sprig.

Place
Morristown, NJ
Scope
Seal, raw-bar card, neon, site
Palette
Slate · salt · cedar

Listen

Inland, but the ice is honest

The bar sits off the Green. The owners had a good raw program and a logo that looked like a beach rental. We stood the shuck line on a Thursday, listed every East Coast name that sold, and wrote a mark that could live on a zinc counter.

The plus sign is the only decoration. The rest is ice and type.

Shape

Shell, tide, cedar ink

Newsreader for the wordmark, IBM Plex Sans for the raw-bar card. The seal is an oyster with cedar needles — stamped tide-green on salt paper and reverse-cut for the window. The site is a slate card: names, prices, a seat at the zinc.
