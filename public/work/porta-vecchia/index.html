Porta Vecchia

A Chatham trattoria named for the old door on Main Street. The identity had to feel inherited — plaster, brick, and an arch guests already walked through.

Place
Chatham, NJ
Scope
Mark, menu, window, site
Palette
Plaster · brick · olive

Listen

The door was already the name

Porta Vecchia sits behind a painted brick arch that predates the lease. Guests said “the old door” before they said the restaurant. The work was to make that doorway printable — on a menu, a wine list, and the glass.

If the mark cannot sit over a doorway, it is not the mark.

Shape

Arch, olive, straw gold

Playfair Display for the wordmark, Karla for service copy. The seal is an arched porta with an olive sprig — vinyl-cut for the window and foil-stamped on cream paper. Brick cloth, plaster menus, straw gold on the reserve card.
