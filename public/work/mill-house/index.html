The Mill House

A Mendham kitchen that needed a seal as durable as a saw blade — gold on navy, printable on a truck door, a chef coat, and the glass.

Place
Mendham, NJ
Scope
Seal, print, window, site
Palette
Navy · brass · stone

Mark

Saw-tooth millstone, gold on navy

Playfair Display for the wordmark, Allura for “The Kitchen,” Montserrat for service copy. The primary logo is a circular millstone with a toothed rim — the same craftsman language as a trade seal, drawn for hospitality. Rim: Fire · Table · Season.

Print

Gold lockup with phone

Trade card application: millstone seal, stacked wordmark, and a number that can sit on a truck magnet.

Print

Cloth, cap, truck, cards, sign

The seal has to survive a chef coat, a navy cap, a pickup door, a hanging lantern sign, visiting cards, tees, and a leather apron. If it cannot print on all of those, it is not the identity.

Window

Gold leaf on the door

The millstone sits on navy millwork so guests read the kitchen before they read the menu.
