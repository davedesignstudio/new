Bell & Court

A Morristown supper club. The lockup is a gold diamond — BC in one stroke — meant for a black jacket, a window, and a trade card with the phone.

Place
Morristown, NJ
Scope
Monogram, print, window, site
Palette
Black · gold · cream

Mark

Diamond monogram, gold on black

Playfair Display for BELL & COURT, Allura for Kitchen + Bar, Montserrat for the list. The BC diamond is the only picture. Everything else is type, foil, and night cloth.

Applications follow the trade-board logic: cloth first, vehicle, hanging sign, visiting cards, tees, leather, then glass.

Window

Gold leaf on the glass

The diamond holds the sidewalk. Kitchen + Bar sits under it so the room reads as a supper club, not a bar that serves food.
