Hospitality

Zarya Table

A Russian hall identity using the studio’s gold-seal system—serif ZARYA, script Table, wheat wreath, and a quiet Moscow fire-hall tagline.

Text system

Same seal construction as True Olive: ZARYA in gold caps serif, Table in script, and MOSCOW · FIRE · HALL on the arc. The wreath is wheat. The dome is the hall. Black is only the disc so gold reads from the curb.

Challenge

Russian restaurant marks often collapse into costume folk art or a Kremlin postcard. The table needed an imperial seal that could hang on brick at dusk and sit on cream dinner paper without being redrawn.

Approach

We kept the studio’s hospitality order: lockup, street, sheet. Hanging black sign, gold foil, birch and snow outside, warm hall inside. Menu in gold and oxblood on cream. No food photographs. No invented prices.

Outcome

A Moscow fire-hall system ready for signage and dinner service. Studio study, not a live reservation book.

Applications

Logo, window, menu

The same wreath on the disc, the hanging sign, and the dinner sheet—the way True Olive leaves the table.
