Nightjar

A Madison wine bar named for the bird that sings after the street goes quiet. Midnight, plum, and a gold line that can sit on a bottle tag.

Place
Madison, NJ
Scope
Mark, list, glass, site
Palette
Midnight · plum · gold

Listen

After the shops close

Nightjar opens when Waverly empties. The list is short, natural, and rewritten weekly. The existing identity looked like a cocktail lounge. We sat the bar on a Tuesday, listed every grower that sold out, and drew the bird in one stroke.

The bird is the only picture. The list does the rest.

Shape

Flight, gold, midnight stock

Libre Bodoni for the wordmark, Manrope for the pour list. The seal is a nightjar in a circle — gold foil on midnight paper, etched on the stemware. The site is a plum card: what’s open, what to eat, a seat after eight.
