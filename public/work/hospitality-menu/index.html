Hospitality storytelling

Menus, guest touchpoints, and visual direction that make a dining room feel as considered as the food.

Challenge

Great kitchens sometimes hide behind generic menus and mixed messaging. Guests need atmosphere before they taste anything.

Approach

We align naming, menu hierarchy, photography direction, and print finishes so the story holds from reservation to receipt.

Outcome

Touchpoints that feel owned by the restaurant—clearer ordering, stronger memory, and a brand guests recognize offline and online.
