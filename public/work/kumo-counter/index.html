Kumo Counter

Eight seats on South Street. Kumo means cloud — the service is quiet, seasonal, and over before the block notices. The identity had to whisper and still hold a door.

Place
Morristown, NJ
Scope
Seal, washi menu, noren, site
Palette
Sumi · indigo · vermillion

Listen

A room that should not advertise

The chef wanted a reservation site and a mark guests would remember on chopsticks — not a logo that shouted sushi. We ate the counter, watched the pass, and wrote the name in both scripts before drawing anything.

The cloud is the only picture. Everything else is type and paper.

Shape

Indigo paper, one vermillion chop

Shippori Mincho for the wordmark, Zen Kaku Gothic for hours and reservations. The seal is a three-curve cloud in a circle, stamped vermillion on washi menus and reverse-printed on the noren. The site is almost a card: date, seats, a sentence.
