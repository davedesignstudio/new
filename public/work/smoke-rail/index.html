Brand identity kit

Smoke Rail

Rail-yard barbecue identity: industrial weight up top, script warmth in the middle, yard tagline below.

Text system

Built on the caps–script wordmark hierarchy: SMOKE in caps serif, Rail in script, and RAIL · YARD · BARBECUE in small caps—so the mark reads as craft and premium at every size.

Challenge

BBQ brands skew either too rustic or too loud—few systems survive menu, merch, and delivery van together.

Approach

The caps–script text stack gave Smoke Rail authority and craft in one mark, then we applied it across campaign and vehicle graphics.

Outcome

A bronze-on-black system that smells like smoke and reads like a serious yard—without losing the hospitality smile in the script.

Applications

Logo, poster, tee, van

One lockup carried across campaign, merch, and vehicle so the brand feels like a system—not a single file.
