Linden Oven

A Madison bakery named for the trees on the street. Flour-dusted paper, a heart-leaf seal, and type that can sit on a brown bag without looking precious.

Place
Madison, NJ
Scope
Leaf mark, bags, board, site
Palette
Flour · cocoa · terracotta

Listen

Morning trade, not a café brand

Linden Oven sells bread first. Coffee is the second sentence. The existing signage looked like a juice bar. We stood the Saturday line, listed every loaf that sold out, and built a mark that could stamp a kraft bag at speed.

If it cannot print on a bag, it is not the identity.

Shape

Leaf, flour, terracotta

Fraunces for the wordmark, Nunito Sans for the board. The linden leaf is a single stroke — heart-shaped, veined, easy to vinyl-cut. Terracotta ink on flour paper; cocoa type on the window. The site is a morning board: hours, loaves, wholesale.
