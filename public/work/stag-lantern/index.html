Stag Lantern

A Bernardsville wood grill. The seal is a stag and a lantern in a toothed gold ring — built to sit on a navy shirt, a hanging inn sign, and the door.

Place
Bernardsville, NJ
Scope
Seal, print, window, site
Palette
Navy · brass · charcoal

Mark

Stag, lantern, toothed gold ring

Playfair Display for STAG LANTERN, Allura for The Grill, Montserrat for hours. Rim copy: Grill · Wine · Fire. The lantern is the only light in the mark — everything else is antler and type.

Print

Gold lockup with phone

Seal, wordmark, script, and (908) 555-0194 on matte black — the same trade-card logic as a design-and-build lockup, written for a grill.

Print

Shirt, cap, truck, sign, lantern

The toothed ring has to hold a chest, a hat, a door panel, a hanging board, cards, tees, and a leather apron. Built on skill. Lit by fire.

Window

Gold leaf on the inn door

The stag and lantern sit on navy millwork with a real lantern beside the glass.
