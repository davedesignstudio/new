Hari

Modern Indian on Springfield Avenue. Hari means green — the cooking is bright, the room is night, and the mark is a marigold that can hold a tandoor menu.

Place
Summit, NJ
Scope
Seal, menu, window, site
Palette
Night · saffron · turmeric

Listen

Not a buffet brand

The kitchen wanted a name that read as contemporary without flattening the food into “fusion.” We ate the tandoor, listed every chaat that left the pass, and drew a marigold before we touched type. The room is dark so the plates can be loud.

Saffron is the only hot color. Everything else is night and cream.

Shape

Marigold, night paper, leaf green

Cormorant Garamond for the wordmark, Outfit for hours and reservations. The seal is an eight-petal marigold — foil turmeric on night stock, vinyl gold on the glass. The site is a cream card against charcoal: the menu, then a table.
