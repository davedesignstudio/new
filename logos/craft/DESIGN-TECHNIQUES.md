# Design techniques — Philhower and Okrogly Design and Build craft canon

Plain language. Labeled shelves. Pressure-tested work beats polish theater.

**History shelf:** `DESIGN-HISTORY-1950-NOW.md` (site: `/history/`) — design from 1950 to now on parallel labeled shelves.

## Design techniques

**GenUI envelope contract + partial-safe streaming**

- **Envelope contract:** Every generated UI chunk declares what it is (kind), what it may change (scope), and what it must not invent (constraints) before content streams.
- **Partial-safe streaming:** Incomplete replies stay usable — show Action + Item + Limit status; never require a finished payload to be honest.
- **Limit:** Stream polish last; stream stakes and constraints first.

## Principles / breaks

**Front-load stakes; never stage away danger/legal**

- Put risk, cost, liability, and legal edges in the first pass — not Act III.
- Do not “stage away” danger with soft framing, euphemism, or decorative delay.
- Break rule: if a mockup hides the hard part, the mockup is wrong.

## Art

**Objecthood + Afrofuturist craft; no civilizational ladder**

- Treat marks and materials as *objects* with weight, join, and use — not flat decoration.
- Draw on Afrofuturist craft: speculative, technical, ancestral, made — without ranking cultures on a “progress” ladder.
- Limit: no civilizational hierarchy aesthetics (primitive → modern → future). Parallel crafts, labeled shelves.

## Writing

**Plain ≠ meagre; Action + Item + Limit status**

- Plain writing carries full meaning; short is not empty.
- Status format: **Action** (what moved) + **Item** (what it touched) + **Limit** (what it does not claim).
- Example: *Updated* work index · *three identity kits* · *case studies only; no pricing*.

## Jesus / Bible / canons

**Mt 7 hear+do; shelves labeled (Meqabyan ≠ Maccabees)**

- Matthew 7: hearing without doing is unfinished craft — apply the brief, don’t only quote it.
- Keep canons on **labeled shelves**: Ethiopian Meqabyan is not the Greek Maccabees; do not collapse names or traditions.
- Limit: reverence through accuracy of citation and practice, not brand cosplay.

## Craftsmanship

**Cooperage rive/saw logic; pressure-test ≠ polish**

- Cooperage logic: some wood is *riven* (split with the grain), some is *sawn* (cut across). Choose the method that fits the stave — don’t force one tool for every job.
- Pressure-test the joint under load before polishing the surface.
- Limit: shine without stress-test is unfinished work.
