## Research capsules (this hour)
### Design techniques / styles
Crosslink treats **control-plane fields** (croze rows, vault schema) as design material equal to type and color. GenUI lesson from prior passes: marked fields are the envelope; photography is fill.

### Design principles — when to break
- **Break proximity / progressive disclosure** for irreversible-adjacent risks (allergens, hold expiry, authenticity).
- **Keep progressive disclosure** for optional filters and secondary marketing.
- First viewport may include a stakes strip (intentional hero-budget break justified by care).

### Art as whole
Looking regimes: hospitality sites invite; Sole Archive labels. Place/object credit prevents floating stock imagery from becoming uncredited appropriation.

### Writing styles through time
A+I+L (Action · Item · Limit) compresses panic status into plain speech—kin to didactic plain style and modern UX microcopy without meagreness.

### Jesus / Bible / canons
Didache Two Ways × Good Samaritan: stakes copy is neighbor-care, not CYA panic. Hear+do rock: allergen/hold truth must be *done* (spoken back / printed on card), not only displayed.

### Craftsmanship
Croze metaphor from cooperage: the groove that seats the head. Guest safety and hold expiry are the seating grooves of service craft.
