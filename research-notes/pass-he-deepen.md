## Design techniques & styles (deepened)

### Kinetic / variable type as *layout*
Survey named kinetic type as a 2026 marketing option. Deepen: type is not decoration on a layout — **oversized brand/headline *is* the composition**. Variable axes (weight/width) may breathe on scroll/hover only when they reinforce hierarchy. Constraints carried forward:
- Brand-need-first (cafe warmth ≠ archive severity)
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` cancels drift/weight animation
- Envelope chrome (nav, stakes, live status) stays calm; kinetic lives in fill/hero

### Organic / anti-grid as *fill*
Asymmetry, diagonal rhythm, and soft section dividers belong in storytelling fill — menus, vault grids, terrace narratives. Utility surfaces (Hold, Reserve, allergen stakes) keep structured scan paths. Anti-minimalism ≠ clutter: personality without burying danger.
