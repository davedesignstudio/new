The Room Method

Sit → Say → Scatter → Shear → Stage → Ship

An original studio process for hospitality identity and the web as one system. Discovery on the property. Golden’s cut before anything goes public. Print and screen share the same language.

01 · Sit Be in the problem

We come to you. Eat, walk the room, stand at the window. Capture who holds the mark and who sees it from the sidewalk.

02 · Say One line

The sentence the window must say. The job the homepage must finish. You correct the brief. We do not invent a brand you did not say.

03 · Scatter Many answers

Twenty thumbnails. Wordmarks, symbols, seals. Quantity first. No polishing until the shortlist is three.

04 · Shear Golden’s cut

Reduce to geometry. One idea. One color must work. Favicon and window. Kill decoration that does not carry meaning.

05 · Stage Prove it on holds

Menu, window, pack, card, phone — same system. If it breaks on a coaster or a phone screen, it is not done.

06 · Ship Hand off and listen

Print files, guidelines, the site. We stay until the room, the board, and the phone match.
