Cala

Table on the water

  • Identity
    Studio study — coastal table
  • Services
    Identity · menu · packaging · print
  • Year
    2026
  • Place
    Mediterranean coast

Cala is Catalan and Spanish for cove. The inks come from the site: limewashed plaster, Aegean teal, sunbaked terracotta, olive leaf, lemon, sand. No black-and-gold café costume. No boat clipart. The table faces the water, so the palette does too.

The mark is an architectural arch — a cove you walk through — terracotta on the rim, sea in the fill. CALA in a high-contrast serif. TABLE ON THE WATER in a grotesque, small enough to survive a napkin band. One construction at four scales: wall, glass, menu, pocket. This is a studio study, not a live job.

Print system

Color from the cove

Same arch on the wall, the window, the menu, and the matchbook. The location supplies the inks. Print is how the table leaves the terrace.
