Bville Pizza & Grill

The town’s nickname, as a lockup

In-house graphic design, 2018–2021. Branding, menus, and the website — one construction from the lot to the lid.

Locals already shorten Bernardsville to Bville. That is the brand. Not an invented word, not a pizza pun. The town saying its own name, written so it can sit on glass.

David was the graphic designer in the building from 2018 to 2021: branding, menus, and the website. This is the lockup from that work — orange B, green ville, gold edge, two swooshes on slate — used as the logo with no redraw. The B is appetite and oven heat from the lot. Ville sits in forest green so the town and the grill are in the name, not a second pizza red.

Pizza & Grill are not a slogan under a pie. They are two appetites under one roof: the pie, and the rest of the plate — pasta, salad, the grill. One lockup has to hold both. No clipart pizza. The food is inside the box. The window is a sign, not a collage. The public site had to carry the same name as the glass: the street, the hours, the order.

Cream is the paper you order from. Orange is the price. Green is the name of the dish. Burgundy is the lid that leaves. Forest green wraps the napkin. Slate is the field the lockup was built on. One split, every scale.

The brand

Six facts the lockup already knows

A complete Bville story is not a moodboard. It is the nickname, the two appetites, the inks, the room on Morristown Road, the paper that leaves, and the years the work was made in the building.

  1. 01 — The nickname

    Bernardsville shortens itself. Bville is how the town talks. The brand is that speech, not a pizza pun and not a second invented name.

  2. 02 — Two appetites

    Pizza and Grill. Pie and plate. Pasta, salad, the rest of the order. One roof. The lockup has to hold both without a clipart slice.

  3. 03 — The split

    Orange B is heat from the lot. Green ville is the town. Gold is the edge of the sign. Two swooshes close the field. Slate underneath so the colors read at dusk.

  4. 04 — The room

    159 Morristown Road. The lockup on glass. Cream hours because the menu is cream. A bench on the sidewalk. Guests meet the brand before they meet the pie.

  5. 05 — The paper

    Cream menu, cream loyalty, forest napkin, burgundy lid. Ten pies, one on the house. The same slate lockup, from shop scale to pocket scale.

  6. 06 — The years

    2018–2021, in-house. Branding, menus, and the website were one job. The public site had to say Bville the way the window does: street, hours, order — not a template with a new logo dropped in.

The trail: keep the uploaded lockup. Keep the split. Do not add a pizza drawing. The food is inside.

Print system

How Bville leaves the building

Window, menu, lid, card, napkin. The same lockup — not a circular white-script substitute. Print is the story at the scale a guest actually holds.
