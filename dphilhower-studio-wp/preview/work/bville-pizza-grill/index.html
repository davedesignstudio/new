Bville Pizza & Grill

Bernardsville, shortened the way locals say it

  • Client
    Bville Pizza & Grill
  • Role
    In-house graphic designer
  • Years
    2018–2021
  • Place
    Bernardsville, NJ

David was the graphic designer at Bville Pizza & Grill from 2018 to 2021: branding, menus, and the website. This is the lockup from that work — orange B, green ville, gold edge, two swooshes on slate — used as the logo with no redraw.

Locals already shorten Bernardsville to Bville. The orange B is appetite and oven heat from the lot. Ville sits in forest green so the town and the grill are in the name, not a second pizza red. No clipart pizza: the food is inside the box.
