Bville Pizza & Grill

Bernardsville, shortened the way locals say it

  • Sample
    Studio sample, Bernardsville pizza and grill
  • Services
    Identity · menu · packaging
  • Year
    2025
  • Place
    Bernardsville, NJ

Locals already shorten Bernardsville to Bville. The mark has to work as a script on slate and as one ink hit on kraft.

The orange B is appetite and oven heat — the first letter you see from the lot. Ville sits in forest green so the town and the grill are in the name, not a second pizza red. A gold outline lifts the script off dark slate like a physical sign. The swooshes are Friday-night energy without a clipart pizza.

How Bville leaves the building

Orange for heat, green for the town, gold so it reads as a sign. Print follows that split so the menu, the box, and the window are one voice.
