Room Method

From leaf to glass

Aurea was sheared in the Room Method: one gold-leaf idea that reads on black, on cream, and on glass — then staged on every hold.

01 · Sit A room that wants leaf

Who stays for primi, who takes oil home, and what has to live on glass in gold leaf — not a flag, not clipart pizza.

02 · Say Trattoria · Forno · Vino

One line the window can hold. Black is the plate. Gold is the leaf. Teal is the diamond that frames the monogram.

03 · Scatter Twenty gold answers

Monograms, seals, Trajan arcs, blackletter locks. Quantity first. No favorites until Shear.

04 · Shear One leaf, one idea

Ornate gold monogram on black. AUREA in the same leaf. Thin teal diamond. Kill every second idea.

05 · Stage Seal, menu, pack, phone

Prove the mark on glass, cream list, oil and ragù, cards, coasters, and a homepage that uses the same black and gold.

06 · Ship One kit, every surface

Print files, naming, and a system the room can run. Studio identity — Morris County, NJ.
