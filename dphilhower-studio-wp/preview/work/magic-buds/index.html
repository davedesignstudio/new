Sleep is the product. The photograph sells the result; the label has to feel calm and expensive — not like a party and not like a clinic.

High-key white is trust and quiet. Deep purple is night without looking like a head shop. The script and small stars make “magic” approachable. There is no cannabis leaf: this is rest. Gold on purple is the only luxury move, so a shelf can hold it next to bigger brands.
