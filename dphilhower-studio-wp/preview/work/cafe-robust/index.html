Cafe Robust

The board you read before you order

  • Sample
    Studio sample, neighborhood coffee shop
  • Services
    Identity · menu · packaging · window
  • Year
    2026
  • Place
    Morris County, NJ

A chalkboard that tries to look handmade usually looks cheap. This one is drawn as a sign: gold filigree, a cup from above, and a list you can read from the door.

The circular CR mark is the drink seen from above — the same ring as the foam. Tan script is premium without becoming wedding stationery. Prices sit in two columns so 16 and 20 don’t fight the names. Cups and the window carry the same seal so the board is not a one-off poster.
