Cafe Robust

The board you read before you order

  • Identity

  • Services
    Identity · menu · packaging · window
  • Year
    2026
  • Place

A chalkboard that tries to look handmade usually looks cheap. This one is drawn as a sign: gold filigree, a cup from above, and a list you can read from the door.

The official seal is the crema ring: gold CR, silver CAFE / ROBUST, stars, and a foam border. That badge sits on the cups and the bag. The board and window keep the cup-from-above so the shop still reads from the sidewalk.
