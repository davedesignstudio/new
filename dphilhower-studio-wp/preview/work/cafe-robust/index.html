Cafe Robust

The board you read before you order

  • Identity
    neighborhood coffee shop
  • Services
    Identity · menu · packaging · window
  • Year
    2026
  • Place
    Morris County, NJ

A chalkboard that tries to look handmade usually looks cheap. This one is drawn as a sign: gold filigree, a cup from above, and a list you can read from the door.

The official seal is the crema ring: gold CR, silver CAFE / ROBUST, stars, and a foam border. That badge sits centered on the cups. The bag is a finished retail pack — House Blend, dark roast, whole bean, net weight, origin, tasting notes, roast date, Nutrition Facts, ingredients, brew, lot, and barcode. The board and window keep the cup-from-above so the shop still reads from the sidewalk.
