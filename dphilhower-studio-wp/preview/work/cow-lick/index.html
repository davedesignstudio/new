The cow licks. That is the brief. If the drawing is not funny, the name is just two words.

The tongue is literal so you remember it. A thick sticker halo — brown, terracotta, cream — exists so the same die-cut can live on a pint, a window, and a freezer door. Chocolate-to-tan type reads as ice cream, not a farm NGO. Terracotta is the patches, the cone, and the warmth.
