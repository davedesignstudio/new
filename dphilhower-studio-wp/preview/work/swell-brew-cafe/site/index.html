The cafe

A coastal cup. The swell lives in the rim.

Swell Brew Cafe sits where a wave can fit in a mug. Guests meet the lockup on the wall, then the cream board, then whatever the bar wrote that day. Stay for the cup. That is the whole brief.

  1. The mug

    Seen from above, handle as a tab. The cafe is a cup before it is a picture of the sea.

  2. The swell

    Navy wave in the rim, pink light in the curl. Weather you can print on a sleeve.

  3. The room

    Whitewash, navy frames, pine, the water in the glass. The lockup stays small on the wall.

  4. The paper

    Cream stock, two columns, empty lines until the bar speaks. Hours you can read without a PDF.
