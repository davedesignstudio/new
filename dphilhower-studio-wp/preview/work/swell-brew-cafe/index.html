Swell Brew Cafe

The swell is in the cup

Studio study. The uploaded lockup, a cream cafe board, and the site — one construction from the mug to the paper the guest reads before they order.

The mark was uploaded as a print on paper, so that print is the identity. A mug seen from above. The handle is a tab. Inside the rim, a swell — a wave, not a clipart surfboard and not a second coffee bean. Dusty pink light sits in the curl. Rounded Swell Brew over Cafe in the same navy. This is not Ritual’s broken ring, not EXPresso’s pour, and not a lantern on the shore.

Navy is the cup and the name. Foam pink is the light in the wave. Sand is the paper. Cream is the board you order from. No invented street. No invented years in-house. No invented drinks: the kitchen has not given a list yet, so the board keeps ruled lines and two honest headers, The cup / The oven.

The system

Navy mug. Wave inside. Cream board.

The uploaded lockup is the whole identity. Mug, wave, and wordmark stay together. The cafe board steals a two-column cream construction and keeps Swell Brew weather — navy, foam, sand. Nothing is a substitute circular bean stamp.

The mug is the cafe. The swell is the weather. Pink is only the light in the curl. Cream is the sheet. Navy is the ink that survives a sleeve.

The cafe

Six facts the mug already knows

A complete Swell Brew story is not a moodboard. It is the mug, the swell, the three inks, the room that faces the water, the cream board, and the fact that this is a study — not a live line at the register.

  1. 01 — The mug

    Seen from above, handle as a tab. The cafe is a cup before it is a wave. That is how the mark reads at sleeve scale.

  2. 02 — The swell

    The wave lives inside the rim. Not a surfboard parked on the glass. The weather of the coast, held in the drink.

  3. 03 — The split

    Navy is the name and the cup. Foam pink is only the light in the curl. Sand is the paper. Three inks — no extras for luxury, none dropped to make a black void.

  4. 04 — The room

    Whitewash, navy frames, pine counter, the sea in the glass. The lockup is small on the wall. Guests meet that weather before they meet the board.

  5. 05 — The paper

    Cream cafe board. Centered lockup. Two columns. Double border. The cup / The oven. Ruled lines, no invented drinks, no prices until they are given. Navy and cream cups are the same lockup, hand scale.

  6. 06 — The study

    2026, studio study — not a live job, not a street address, not years in-house. Branding, the board, and the cafe site are one construction: name, hours, order — when the bar writes the order.

The trail: keep the uploaded lockup. Keep the swell in the cup. Do not invent the drinks. The food is in the oven when the kitchen says so.
