Noche Roja is a studio cantina system written for a room that wants fire on the table, not a flag on the wall. Sit at the mesa, write Cantina · Fuego · Mesa, draw the NR seal, then put that badge on glass, the cream list, the salsa jar, and the phone.

Chili is the heat. Adobe is the wall. Ink is the night. Marigold is the physical stamp. No food photography on the menu. This is a studio identity, not a live account.

Full process

From the mesa to the fuego

A cantina system written for dusk and a red door. Sit down, write the line the window can hold, draw the marigold seal, then print cream, chili, and kraft until takeaway still reads as Noche Roja.

01 · Sit down A table under the string lights

The first conversation is who walks in for tacos, who stays for mezcal, and what has to live on glass in marigold — not a cartoon sombrero.

02 · Write it back Cantina · Fuego · Mesa

The brief: chili for heat, adobe for the wall, ink for night, marigold for the stamp. The food stays inside. The line under the seal has to agree with the menu and the jar.

03 · Draw Marigold NR, chili plate

Official circular seal: marigold NR, NOCHE ROJA on the top arc, CANTINA on the bottom, chili and sun at the sides. Quiet serif for the list. Marigold rules.

04 · Build Window, list, pack, phone

Dusk window, cream menu, salsa and takeaway, cards, coasters, loyalty, poster, matchbook, and a homepage that uses the same chili and marigold. Studio identity — Morris County, NJ.
