The Hills already looks like money. The mark had to hold grit and glamour: a saw blade in bronze, not a colonial seal in navy.

The blade is the tool — precision, wood, the work. Bronze instead of hazard orange elevates a contractor to a studio the house can live with on the lawn. Serif SERVICE is authority. Script The Hills is the place. The curved tagline Renovate Remodel Restore makes a seal, not a flyer.
