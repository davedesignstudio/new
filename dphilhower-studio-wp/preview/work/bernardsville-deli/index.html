Warm deli, cool grocery

Two businesses, one town name. Warm gradients for food you eat now, blue for the aisle you shop later.

Deli in orange-to-pink is appetite. Bernardsville gets the long B flourish — a signature, family-owned, large enough to survive crumpled paper. The blue line is a horizon between the counter and the grocery. Grocery in blue-to-periwinkle is stable retail. The red ampersand is the only loud punctuation.
