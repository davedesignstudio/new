The eastern shore already has limestone and oil. The restaurant is a harbor table. The fruit is the company — the way a saw is the company when the work is wood. This is not Cala’s western cove, and it is not a mosque, a crescent, or a boat sticker. An olive in bronze, not a lantern in gold.

The wreath is the tool. Serif TRUE is authority. Script Olive is the fruit. PRESS TABLE SHORE on the arc makes a seal. Black is only the disc. Cream is the paper you order from. Olive is the name of the dish. Bronze is the rule. The room keeps the rest of the inks: tahini, limestone, lemon, sumac, pomegranate, harbor, copper.

The offer is trays, in the order they were given: grape leaves, shish, kufta, chicken kabob, chicken and beef shawarma, roasted chicken legs, lemon pepper breast, veggie tray, mixed zucchini onions and peppers, oven-roasted lamb, yellow butter rice, basmati. No invented prices. No food photos on the dinner menu — the kitchen is twenty feet away. This is a studio study, not a live job.

The restaurant

Six facts the table already knows

A complete True Olive story is not a moodboard. It is the fruit, the trays, the inks, the limestone room, the cream dinner paper, and the fact that this is a study — not a live reservation book.

  1. 01 — The fruit

    The olive is the company. Not a lantern, not a cove arch, not a decorative script. Serif TRUE, script Olive, bronze wreath on a black disc. Press, table, shore.

  2. 02 — The trays

    Grape leaves, shish, kufta, chicken kabob, chicken and beef shawarma, roasted chicken legs, lemon pepper breast, veggie tray, mixed zucchini onions and peppers, oven-roasted lamb, yellow butter rice, basmati. That is the appetite. Nothing else is invented.

  3. 03 — The split

    Bronze is the seal and the rule. Olive is the name of the dish. Tahini is the paper. Seal black is only the disc. Lemon, sumac, pomegranate, harbor, and copper stay in the room — they are not sanded off for luxury.

  4. 04 — The room

    Eastern harbor. Golden limestone, olive trees, copper light, cream linen. The seal is small on the wall. Guests meet the weather of the table before they meet the tray.

  5. 05 — The paper

    Cream dinner menu. Centered seal. Two columns. Double border. Names in olive, no prices until they are given. No food photographs on the sheet: the kitchen is twenty feet away.

  6. 06 — The study

    2026, studio study — not a live job, not a street address, not years in-house. Branding, the dinner menu, and the restaurant site are one construction, the way a real table would meet you: name, hours, order.

The trail: keep the bronze seal. Keep the trays in order. Do not add a lantern. The food is inside.
