The restaurant

A harbor table. The olive is the company.

True Olive sits on the eastern shore. Limestone, oil, and the water. Guests meet the seal on the wall, then the cream dinner paper, then the tray. Share the table. That is the whole brief.

  1. The fruit

    Bronze wreath on a black disc. Serif TRUE, script Olive, press table shore on the arc. Not a lantern shop, not a western cove.

  2. The trays

    Grape leaves first. Then kabob, shawarma, roast, veg, rice. The kitchen sends the plate; the paper only names it.

  3. The room

    Golden stone, olive trees, copper light, cream linen. Harbor through the arch. The weather stays on the table.

  4. The paper

    Cream stock, two columns, names in olive. No pictures on the dinner menu. Hours you can read without a PDF.

Visit

The eastern harbor. A studio study for a table on the Levantine shore — not a live reservation book. The dinner menu and the hours are how a real restaurant would meet you.
