Philhower & O’Krogly

Day and night are the hours, not decoration

  • Sample

  • Services

A contractor who answers at 2am cannot look like a franchise, and cannot look like a country club. The mark had to hold both: craft in daylight, emergency after dark.

The gothic kp ligature sits in a thin ice-blue diamond — two names, one seal. Blue is the night call. Tan-gold is the build. The sun arc and the moon are the hours written as pictures so a magnet on a fridge still reads when the type is too small. Hazard orange would make it a cone. Black keeps it a studio the house can live with on the lawn.
