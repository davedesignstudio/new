Restaurant brand systems

Identity, menus, and storefront presence

  • Client
    Hospitality groups, Morris County
  • Services
    Identity · menus · signage direction
  • Year
    2024
  • Location
    Bernardsville & Morristown, NJ

Hospitality brands need more than a logo. Guests meet the identity on a window, a menu, a takeout bag, and a phone screen—often in the same week.

The work

D Philhower Studio builds restaurant brand systems from the ground up: mark, type, color, menus, and a simple set of rules so staff and printers stay consistent. The goal is a brand that feels local and crafted, not a template retrofitted for the next dining room.

Outcome

A kit of files and guidelines the kitchen, front of house, and website can all share—so the food and the design pull in the same direction.
