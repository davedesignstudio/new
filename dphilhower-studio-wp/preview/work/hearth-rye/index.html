Hearth & Rye is a studio roast-house system written for a room with a fire and a loaf on the board. Sit at the two-top, write Roast · Rye · Fire, draw the HR seal, then put that badge on glass, the cream list, the mustard jar, and the phone.

Charcoal is the room. Rye is the loaf. Cream is the stock. Copper is the physical stamp. No food photography on the menu. This is a studio identity, not a live account.

Full process

From the hearth to the loaf

A roast-house system written for charcoal and copper. Sit down, write the line the window can hold, draw the copper seal, then print cream, charcoal, and kraft until takeaway still reads as Hearth & Rye.

01 · Sit down A booth by the fire

The first conversation is who walks in for a roast, who takes bread home, and what has to live on glass in copper — not a cartoon steak.

02 · Write it back Roast · Rye · Fire

The brief: charcoal for the room, rye for the loaf, cream for stock, copper for the stamp. The food stays inside. The line under the seal has to agree with the menu and the jar.

03 · Draw Copper HR, charcoal plate

Official circular seal: copper HR, HEARTH & RYE on the top arc, ROAST HOUSE on the bottom, wheat and flame at the sides. Quiet serif for the list. Copper rules.

04 · Build Window, list, pack, phone

Dusk window, cream menu, mustard and takeaway, cards, coasters, loyalty, poster, matchbook, and a homepage that uses the same charcoal and copper. Studio identity — Morris County, NJ.
