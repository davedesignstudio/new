Mizu is a studio ramen system written for steam on a wet sidewalk. Sit at the counter, write Ramen · Steam · Night, draw the indigo seal, then put that badge on glass, the cream list, the chili-oil bottle, and the phone.

Indigo is the night. Steam is the stock. Charcoal is the counter. Vermillion is the only loud accent. No food photography on the menu. This is a studio identity, not a live account.

Full process

From the steam to the night

A ramen system written for indigo and steam. Sit at the counter, write the line the window can hold, draw the seal, then print cream, indigo, and kraft until takeaway still reads as Mizu.

01 · Sit down A stool at the counter

The first conversation is who walks in for a bowl, who takes chili oil home, and what has to live on glass in white — not a cartoon noodle.

02 · Write it back Ramen · Steam · Night

The brief: indigo for night, steam for cream stock, charcoal for the counter, vermillion for heat. The food stays inside. The line under the seal has to agree with the menu and the bottle.

03 · Draw White MIZU, indigo plate

Official circular seal: MIZU in condensed white caps, RAMEN on the top arc, STEAM · NIGHT on the bottom, wave motif, thin vermillion ring. Quiet sans for the list.

04 · Build Window, list, pack, phone

Dusk window, cream menu, chili oil and takeaway, cards, coasters, loyalty, poster, matchbook, and a homepage that uses the same indigo and vermillion. Studio identity — Morris County, NJ.
