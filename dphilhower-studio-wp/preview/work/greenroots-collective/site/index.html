The restaurant

A garden table. The hands are the roots.

GreenRoots Collective sits where a tree can grow in a pair of palms. Guests meet the lockup on the wall, then the cream dinner paper, then whatever the kitchen wrote that day. Share the table. That is the whole brief.

  1. The hands

    Cupped roots, a seed in the palms. The collective is care — not a second invented name.

  2. The tree

    Forest canopy, brown trunk, yellow sun. Growth you can print on kraft and glass.

  3. The garden

    Wicker, tomatoes, carrots, greens, squash. The board already photographed the table.

  4. The paper

    Cream stock, two columns, empty lines until the kitchen speaks. Hours you can read without a PDF.
