The mark was uploaded as a board, so the board is the identity. A tree with cupped-hand roots and a seed in the palms. A yellow sun behind the trunk. Script GreenRoots. Caps COLLECTIVE. The sentence under it is the offer: Growing Better, Together. This is not a lantern, not a pizza pun, and not a costume farm alphabet dropped onto a template.

Forest green is the name of the tree. Earth brown is the hands and the rule. Creamy yellow is the sun. Cream is the paper you order from. Kraft is the tag that leaves on burlap. The garden still on the board — tomatoes, carrots, greens, squash in a wicker basket — is the table, photographed. No invented street. No invented years in-house. No invented dishes: the kitchen has not given a list yet, so the dinner paper keeps ruled lines and two honest headers, From the garden / From the kitchen.

The restaurant

Six facts the tree already knows

A complete GreenRoots story is not a moodboard. It is the hands, the tree, the three inks, the garden basket on the board, the cream paper, and the fact that this is a study — not a live reservation book.

  1. 01 — The hands

    The roots are cupped palms. The collective is care, not a second invented name. A seed sits in the hands so growth has somewhere to start.

  2. 02 — The tree

    Green canopy, brown trunk, yellow sun. The fruit of the mark is a plant you can hold, not a lantern and not a clipart tractor.

  3. 03 — The split

    Forest green is the name. Earth brown is the hands and the rule. Creamy yellow is the sun. Three inks on the uploaded board — no extras for luxury, none dropped to make a black void.

  4. 04 — The garden

    The board already photographed the table: wicker, tomatoes, carrots with their tops, greens, squash on straw. Guests meet that weather before they meet a dish list.

  5. 05 — The paper

    Cream market-table menu. Centered lockup. Two columns. Double border. From the garden / From the kitchen. Ruled lines, no invented plates, no prices until they are given. Kraft tag on burlap is the same lockup, pocket scale.

  6. 06 — The study

    2026, studio study — not a live job, not a street address, not years in-house. Branding, the dinner paper, and the restaurant site are one construction: name, hours, order — when the kitchen writes the order.

The trail: keep the uploaded lockup. Keep the three inks. Do not invent the menu. The food is in the basket.
