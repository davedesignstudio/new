A Russian hall identity using the studio’s gold-seal system: serif ZARYA, script Table, wheat wreath, onion-dome mark, and MOSCOW · FIRE · HALL on the arc. The wreath is wheat. The dome is the hall. Black is only the disc so gold reads from the curb.

Same hospitality order as True Olive: lockup, street, sheet. Hanging black sign, gold foil, birch and snow outside, warm hall inside. Menu in gold and oxblood on cream. No food photographs. No invented prices. Studio study, not a live reservation book.
