Black Olive is a studio meze system written for a whitewashed room that wants an olive branch on glass, not a flag. Sit at the two-top, write Meze · Olive · Sea, draw the BO seal, then put that badge on the dusk window, the cream list, the oil bottle, and the phone.

Olive is the grove. Black is the night. Whitewash is the wall. Aegean is the water. No food photography on the menu. This is a studio identity, not a live account.

Full process

From the grove to the sea

A meze system written for whitewash and olive green. Sit down, write the line the window can hold, draw the BO seal, then print cream, olive, and kraft until takeaway still reads as Black Olive.

01 · Sit down A table in the whitewash

The first conversation is who walks in for meze, who takes oil home, and what has to live on glass in cream — not a cartoon column.

02 · Write it back Meze · Olive · Sea

The brief: olive for the grove, black for night, whitewash for the wall, Aegean for water. The food stays inside. The line under the seal has to agree with the menu and the bottle.

03 · Draw Cream BO, olive plate

Official circular seal: cream BO, BLACK OLIVE on the top arc, MEZE on the bottom, olive branch at the sides. Quiet serif for the list. Aegean rules.

04 · Build Window, list, pack, phone

Dusk window, cream menu, oil and takeaway, cards, coasters, loyalty, poster, matchbook, and a homepage that uses the same olive and Aegean. Studio identity — Morris County, NJ.
