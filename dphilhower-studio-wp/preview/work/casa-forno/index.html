An Italian trattoria lockup using the studio’s carved-plaster system: serif CASA, script Forno, terracotta arch, olive sprig, and CUCINA · FIRE · TABLE in small caps. The arch is the oven. The olive is the kitchen.

Same construction as Cala and True Olive: one lockup, then the street, then the sheet the guest holds. Window gold on dusk stucco. Menu in terracotta and olive on cream. No food photographs on the paper — the kitchen is twenty feet away. Studio study, not a live job.
