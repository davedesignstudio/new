Design system

Build the brand. Take the order. Print the ticket.

Five layers, one restaurant. WooCommerce records the pickup order. The kitchen ticket is sent to the printer at the restaurant when the order is placed.

01

Identity

Lock the mark, the colors, and the type. The same kit has to read on the window, the menu, and the phone.

02

Menu

Categories, kitchen names, and prices. The server owns the price. The cart only sends a sku and a quantity.

03

Order

WooCommerce takes the order: guest name, phone, ready window, and a note. Pickup. No shipping.

04

Ticket

One kitchen ticket per order. Item, quantity, total, and the note. Nothing else.

05

Printer

The ticket is sent to the printer at the restaurant (port 9100) when the order is placed. A second status change does not print it again.
