About the studio

David Philhower is a freelance graphic designer. Color and layout are the tools. Every job is a different problem, so every solution has to be different.

Most of the work happens without a computer — pencil, marker, and paper first. Files come last. That is how a restaurant brand stays itself instead of inheriting the last shop’s template.

The studio specializes in small businesses, especially restaurants: identity, menus, packaging, and websites built from the ground up. Design is a labor of love, and so is food. You make the good food. We’ll design a brand that can stand next to it.

From 2018 to 2021 David was the graphic designer at Bville Pizza & Grill in Bernardsville — branding, menus, and the website. Freelance practice as D Philhower design has run since 2019. Associate of Arts in Graphic Design, Sussex County Community College, 2017–2019.

Work comes from Bernardsville, Morristown, and nearby Morris County towns that want a local partner, not a mill that retrofits one layout for the next restaurant.
