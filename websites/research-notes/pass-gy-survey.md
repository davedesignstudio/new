## 5. Craftsmanship — Survey

**Cooperage (literal hoop & stave):**
- Staves jointed on long inverted jointer; angle (“shot”) sets belly and seal.
- **Raising up:** staves assembled inside a temporary hoop (jig).
- Heat / fire softens; winch draws shape; croze cuts groove for heads.
- Driving hoops toward the bilge tightens seams — tightness is geometry, not glue.

**Design metaphor (Survey):**
- Hoop = envelope chrome (nav, stakes, status).
- Staves = content fill (menus, vault pulls).
- Raising up = launch with constraints before heating creativity.
- Unmarked GenUI variants = stave without hallmark → reject (guild logic).

## 6. Design principles & when to break them — Survey

| Principle | KEEP when | BREAK when (disclose) |
|-----------|-----------|------------------------|
| Progressive disclosure | Optional filters, advanced vault metadata | Allergy, authenticity, pier hazard, binding hold — **stakes above fold** |
| Consistency | Envelope chrome across pages | Local seasonal catch board may vary fill |
| Fitts’s law | Primary Hold/Reserve ≥48px | Never shrink emergency/hold CTAs for aesthetics |
| Aesthetic-usability | Calm heroes | Do not pretty-lie about reseller status |
| Cards | Interaction containers (index chooser, vault pick) | Never in hero |

Fruit test: if breaking helps the neighbor (safety, truth, consent), disclose and proceed; else KEEP.
