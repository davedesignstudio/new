# Design Studio delivery — Pass GX

Five-hour research cycle **GT→GX** closed with Integrate fruit applied to five brand websites.

## Research topics covered
- Contemporary design techniques & styles (GenUI envelopes, elastic primitives)
- Art as a whole (looking ethics, place credit, simultaneous traditions)
- Writing styles through time (plain language → Action+Item+Limit microcopy)
- Jesus, biblical teachings, and canons (66 / 73 / LXX+ / Ethiopian ~81)
- Craftsmanship (shu-ha-ri, sashimono, cooperage hoop/stave)
- Design principles and when to break them (KEEP/BREAK canon)

Notes: `research-notes/synthesis-pass-gx.md`
