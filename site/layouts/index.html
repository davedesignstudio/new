02 — Method

Gestalt is how people see. We design for that, not around it.

  1. Proximity Menus group what belongs together so a guest never hunts.
  2. Similarity Type, color, and crop repeat until the brand feels inevitable.
  3. Continuity The eye follows a path from sign to host stand to table.
  4. Closure A mark can be incomplete and still lock in the mind.
  5. Figure / Ground What advances and what recedes is a design decision.
  6. Prägnanz The simplest true form wins in a loud dining room.
