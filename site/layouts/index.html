Our philosophy

Three quiet virtues

{{ partial "svg/lotus" . }}

A Swedish sense of measure, a Japanese love of emptiness, and a Vedic devotion to the pure essence of things — three traditions, one table.

Lagom

lagom — just enough

The Swedish measure of sufficiency. Nothing excess, nothing wanting — portions, seasoning and light all held in quiet proportion.

Ma

間 — the space between

The Japanese reverence for negative space. What is left unsaid on the plate lets each ingredient speak in its own voice.
