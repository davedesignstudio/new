Three lineages, one table

A house of taste

Rasa is the Sanskrit word for juice, essence, and the flavor of an experience. We cook where northern light, Japanese restraint, and Vedic hospitality meet.

Stillhet

Sweden

Lagom: not too little, not too much. Birch, salt, rye, and the long light. A hearth, a linen cloth, and what the forest and the cold sea offer in season.

२

間

Japan

Ma: the space that makes the form. Shun, the peak of a thing. Knife, charcoal, rice, and the empty bowl. Craft as quiet devotion.

The guest is god. Spice as medicine, ghee as light, the six flavors held in balance. Agni — the fire that transforms grain, milk, and offering into a meal.
