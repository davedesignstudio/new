01

Proximity

Things that sit close are read as a group. On a menu, that is category, price, and the dish that belongs with them. On a site, it is title, lede, and services sharing one region so the project does not fall apart.

In the room: specials live with specials. The guest should never cross a desert of space to find the wine that matches the plate.

02

Similarity

Shared shape, weight, or color is a silent “these belong.” A restaurant brand that changes type on every card is asking the guest to relearn the room each time.

In the room: the takeout bag, the window vinyl, and the Instagram tile use one crop, one red, one wordmark.

03

Continuity

The eye prefers a path. We build one from the fascia to the host stand to the menu to the check presenter. Alignment is not decoration. It is how a guest moves.

In the room: the wordmark on the awning sits on the same axis as the mark on the menu. The line continues even when the surface changes.

04

Closure

The mind completes what is almost there. A mark can leave a gap and still lock. Work frames on this site are open on one side for the same reason — the picture finishes itself.

In the room: a letterform that opens toward the plate. A window decal that does not need a full box to read as a sign.

05

Figure and ground

What advances and what recedes is a choice. Black type on cream is one reading. Cream type in a dark field is another. We decide which is the dish and which is the table.

In the room: a bright plate on a dark bar. A quiet wall behind a loud specials board. Contrast is hospitality.

06

Prägnanz

People see the simplest stable form. A dining room is already loud. The identity has to reduce, not add. If a mark needs a paragraph to explain, it is not ready for a 40-second wait at the host stand.

In the room: one wordmark, one accent, one rhythm of space. Complexity lives in the food. The brand stays lucid.

07

Focal point

A field needs one thing that is unlike the rest. On this site it is the rust call to action. On a menu it is the dish you want to sell tonight. If everything shouts, nothing is seen.

In the room: one special, one color, one light over the bar. The rest supports.
