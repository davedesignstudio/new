How it works

Fixed fees, short timelines, no surprises

How long does a project take?
A menu refresh is usually two to three weeks. A full identity with menus and a website runs eight to twelve, depending on how quickly we can get feedback between services.
What does it cost?
Every project is quoted as a fixed fee before we start, based on the scope we agree together. No hourly surprises. Half up front, half on delivery.
Do you only work with restaurants?
Mostly, and by choice. But cafés, bars, food trucks, farms, markets and the small businesses that share a street with them are all welcome.
Can you build the sign too?
Yes. Through Okrogly + Philhower Design and Build we can draw, fabricate and install signage and interior elements, or hand finished drawings to your own contractor.
