We design in place and build in the shop. Every project starts with how the room is used, how the light falls, and what the material wants to do. Then we cut.

## What we make

- **Kitchens and pantries** — shaker, inset, and slab work in walnut, white oak, and painted maple. Hardware that will still feel good in twenty years.
- **Built-ins** — libraries, benches, media walls, and window seats that read as architecture, not furniture parked against a wall.
- **Timber** — pergolas, entries, stairs, and exposed joinery where the structure is the finish.
- **Furniture** — tables, casework, and one-off pieces designed with the same drawings as the room they belong to.

## How a build runs

1. **Walk the room.** We measure, photograph, and talk through how you actually live in the space.
2. **Design.** Plans, samples, and a material board. Nothing is ordered until the joints and the grain make sense.
3. **Shop.** Cut, mill, join, sand, finish. You are welcome on Fridays.
4. **Install.** Level, scribe, fasten, and leave the site cleaner than we found it.

Carpentry here is not a catalog. It is design and build — one conversation from the first sketch to the last coat of oil.
