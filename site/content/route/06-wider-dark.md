## Visual hook
Dawn on a sea-cliff. White stone, glass, a beam like a candle. A bun on a paper boat.

## Conflict
The map ends. Hunger still wants a seat it will not share. Sitting is the only invention left.

## Character beat
The trip’s true want surfaces: not arrival, not a Chosen Two, but belonging at a table with enough seats. The Tower has already fallen. Dinner rebuilds The Star.
