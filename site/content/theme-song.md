### Verse 1

Morning light on maple grain,  
Square the line and mark again.  
Hand to chisel, nail to beam —  
We raise the house inside the dream.

### Verse 2

Pixels sharp and letters true,  
Brand the shop the way we do.  
Screen and sawdust, same old creed —  
Make it solid, make it read.

### Chorus

**Philhower & O’Krogly —**  
**Design + Build, from curb to screen.**  
Call the crew at nine-oh-eight,  
We craft the form and frame the fate.

### Verse 3

Joinery tight and kerning clean,  
Van in black and gold between.  
Yard-sign diamond, polo bright —  
One lockup that holds the light.

### Bridge

Northern hills and timber tall,  
Typeface, joint, and bearing wall.  
One good mark, one honest trade —  
Built by hand and carefully made.

### Final chorus

**Philhower & O’Krogly —**  
**Design + Build, forever seen.**  
Gold on black, the diamond blue —  
We build it once, we build it true.
