## Overview

The Catskills Cottage is Gable & Grain's flagship plan for mountain living in Greene, Ulster, Delaware, and Sullivan counties. At 336 sq ft on the main floor plus a 120 sq ft sleeping loft, it meets the practical limits of most NY tiny house and accessory dwelling unit regulations while delivering genuine Victorian character.

Designed for **year-round occupancy** at elevations up to 3,500 ft, this plan accounts for 60 psf ground snow load, -15°F design temperatures, and the freeze-thaw cycles common to the Hudson Valley highlands.

### Zoning & Permits (NY)

> Always verify with your town building department. Rules vary significantly between Woodstock, Windham, Hunter, Margaretville, and surrounding towns.

- **Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs):** Many Catskills towns now allow ADUs on existing residential lots — typically max 900 sq ft
- **Tiny houses on wheels:** Classified as RVs in NY; full-time dwelling often restricted — this plan is designed for **permanent foundation**
- **Setbacks:** Commonly 15' side, 25' front, 25' rear — check your parcel survey
- **Septic:** NY State Dept. of Health approval required; sand filter common in rocky soil
- **Well:** NYS DEC permit for withdrawals over 100,000 gal/day (household wells exempt, but test for yield)

### Recommended Orientation

- **Long axis east-west** — maximizes south glazing for passive solar gain
- **Entry on north or east side** — mudroom shields living space from prevailing northwest winter winds
- **Porch on south or west** — afternoon shade from deciduous trees (oak, maple) reduces summer cooling load
