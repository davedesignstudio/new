Philhower & Okrogly is a construction and design company that treats carpentry and branding as one craft.

We design and build in wood: kitchens, libraries, furniture, and timber structures that are meant to be used hard and look better for it. We also design and build on the web: names, identities, and sites that carry the same load — clear joints, honest materials, nothing decorative that is not also structural.

## Two crafts, one shop

Most studios split the work. An architect draws. A millwork shop cuts. A brand agency names. A web team ships. We keep the drawing and the making in the same room so the brand and the building do not drift apart.

Measure twice. Prototype in pine or in pixels. Then cut for keeps.

## What we believe

- **Joinery over ornament.** If the structure is right, the thing does not need to shout.
- **Materials tell the truth.** Walnut looks like walnut. Type looks like type. We do not fake either.
- **Built to last.** A cabinet that racks is a failure. A website that cannot be edited is the same failure in another material.
- **One standard.** The staircase and the homepage get the same attention.

If you want a partner who can take a project from a pencil sketch to a finished room — and from a finished room to a living brand — you are in the right shop.
