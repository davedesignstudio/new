**The Three Branches** is a short educational site about how the United States Constitution divides federal power among Congress, the President, and the courts.

It is written for students, new citizens, and anyone who wants the map without the textbook. The primary source is the Constitution itself — especially Articles I, II, and III — with the usual landmarks that civics courses rely on: *The Federalist*, *Marbury v. Madison*, and the later amendments that changed how the branches are chosen.

This is not legal advice, not a brief, and not a complete treatise. The federal government is larger than three buildings, and state governments have their own separations of power. Start here, then read the text.
