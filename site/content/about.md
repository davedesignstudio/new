## Design is a labor of love. So is food.

D. Philhower Design is the studio of **David Philhower**, a graphic designer working out of Oak Ridge, New Jersey. The studio started right after college under the name Warped Minds Design, with a group of designers from New York and New Jersey borrowing methods from both the past and the present. The first client was a family pizzeria in Bernardsville that needed a whole new brand. Restaurants have been the focus ever since.

We believe design is more than taking one layout and retrofitting it for the next restaurant. Every project is a different problem, so every solution should be different too. That means starting with the food, the room and the people running it, and only then opening a sketchbook.

## From the page to the wall

Since 2025 David has also worked in design-build with Okrogly + Philhower Design and Build, which means the studio can take a brand off the page and into the room: signage, wall graphics, finishes and the small carpentry that makes a space feel finished. If it needs to be drawn, printed or built, we can help.

## How we work

1. **Discover.** We eat your food, sit in your room and talk to the people who run it. We look at what your regulars already say about you.
2. **Design.** Names, marks, menus, sites and spaces, developed together so nothing feels bolted on.
3. **Deliver.** Print-ready files, an editable website and a short guide so your staff can keep the brand consistent without calling a designer for every update.

## Who we work with

Independent restaurants, cafés, bars and food businesses, mostly in northern New Jersey, and the small businesses that share a street with them. If you have one location and big ambitions, you are exactly who this studio was built for.
