Philhower &amp; Okrogly is a construction and design company. We design and build in two materials that reward the same patience: wood, and the marks people live with on a screen.

## The shop and the studio

**Okrogly** runs the shop — carpentry design and build, millwork, furniture, stairs, built-ins, and the quiet structures that make a house feel finished. **Philhower** runs the studio — web design, identity, and branding for makers who care how something is put together.

We started working as one company because the standard was already the same. A joint that gaps is a layout that slips. A finish that will not take a hand is a homepage that will not take a reader. Measure twice. Cut once. Leave it honest.

## Where we work

The practice is based in Morristown, New Jersey and takes on carpentry, sites, and brands wherever the work wants a careful hand.

## How we start

Bring a room, a product, a name, or a sketch. We will tell you what we would build, what we would leave out, and how long it takes to do it properly.
