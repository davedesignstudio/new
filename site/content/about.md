This site was built as a civic field guide: short enough to read in one sitting, precise enough to cite Articles I, II, and III without flattening them into a schoolhouse poster.

Primary sources behind the pages include the Constitution and its amendments, *The Federalist* (especially Nos. 47, 51, and 78), and *Marbury v. Madison*, 5 U.S. (1 Cranch) 137 (1803). Numbers of House seats and justices are statutory, and are labeled that way.

Return to the [overview](/), or walk the branches in order: [Legislative](/legislative/), [Executive](/executive/), [Judicial](/judicial/), then [Checks](/checks/).
