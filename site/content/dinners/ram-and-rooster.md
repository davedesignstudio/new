Ram & Rooster is the Central Jersey tasting room that punched through statewide and national lists after opening in summer 2024. Chef Sean Yan serves eight New American courses built on Chinese ingredients and technique — lotus-leaf black cod with white asparagus, smoked trout roe, lavender, and lap cheung, for example — and refuses to repeat dishes once a season turns.

Asparagus and corn come back; the plates do not. New Jersey Monthly gave the room a rare four stars; USA Today put it on a national best-restaurants list. Service is described as polished because the kitchen and floor actually like working together.

**Plan it as** the tasting-menu night when you can get to Metuchen. It is not a menu you skim; it is a menu you surrender to.
