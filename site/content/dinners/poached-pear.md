The Poached Pear Bistro is the Shore BYOB that has quietly reached a twelfth anniversary by letting the plates talk. Owners Marc Giordano and chef-brother Scott keep the room visually calm so dishes like pan-seared local grouper in a Provençale sauce with lobster risotto can do the work.

It is modern American, not a themed seafood shack, which is useful in Point Pleasant Beach. Bring the wine; this is classic Jersey BYO fine dining a few blocks from the boardwalk.

**Plan it as** a date night when you want Shore proximity without fried-platter default.
