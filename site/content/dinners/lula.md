Lula opened in January 2025 after chef Conor Browne’s run at Turtle + the Wolf in Montclair. The 54-seat downtown Ridgewood dining room is named for his daughter, and a portion of profits goes to the Spina Bifida Foundation in honor of his son.

The menu is elevated New American with Mediterranean leanings: hot-honey Peking duck-leg, house focaccia with whipped herb butter, and Goffle Road Farm chicken with fingerling potatoes. A lemon meringue tart tends to survive the seasonal swaps.

**Plan it as** a neighborhood date that still feels chef-driven. Regulars treat it like a clubhouse; first-timers should book.
