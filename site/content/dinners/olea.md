Olea opened late last year and immediately became Spring Lake’s dressed-up date default. Chef Bud Carter’s menu splits the difference between steakhouse and pasta house, with playful starters (crab-cake Rangoon, Buffalo octopus) and craft cocktails named for rock stars.

The dessert people describe in detail is the lemon tart: whipped yuzu ganache, a white-chocolate shell, lemon-curd shortbread, toasted meringue, shaped like an actual lemon. Order it even if you think you do not want dessert.

**Plan it as** a Shore date that is not on the boardwalk. Book; the neighborhood noticed.
