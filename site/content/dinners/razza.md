Razza is the pizza dinner that belongs on a fine-dining map. Chef Dan Richer treats flour, water, and yeast as a long project, and the New York Times famously gave the Grove Street room three stars — still an outlier for a pizzeria.

The wood-fired pies are the point: burrata, tomato, and three different Margheritas. Even the bread and cultured butter (from grass-fed Pennsylvania cream) get the same attention. Finish with panna cotta and salted caramel if you still have room.

**Plan it as** a casual-looking night that eats like a destination. Reservations disappear; walk-ins are a sport.
