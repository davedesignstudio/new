Lita is the Jersey trick of walking past a strip mall and into a New York-energy dining room. Executive chef David Viana (a multiple James Beard nominee) and managing partner Neilly Robinson cook an elevated Iberian menu with an all-chef waitstaff explaining the plates.

Order the Portuguese garlic shrimp (camarao alhinho), the bifana pork-shoulder sandwich, and the paella of the day. Finish with Abuela’s apple cake. The open kitchen can be loud; that is the point.

**Plan it as** a date when you want Iberian cooking without a Newark Airport or Hoboken default. Come hungry enough to share.
