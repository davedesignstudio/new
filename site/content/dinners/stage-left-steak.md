Stage Left opened in 1992 and never left the New Brunswick dinner circuit. Dry-aged Prime and Japanese wagyu are the core, with a tableside trick that still works: steak on a hot Himalayan salt brick, so you finish each slice to the doneness you want.

It is a pre-show restaurant in a theater town. The kitchen will get you out by curtain. That combination — serious beef, no circus, a clock that respects the play — is why it remains on statewide lists more than thirty years in.

**Plan it as** steak before a show, or steak with no show and a slower bottle of red.
