Drew’s Bayshore Bistro has been Keyport’s Low Country room since 2005, which is a long run for a cuisine that is not native to Monmouth County. The must-order remains voodoo shrimp: Worcestershire cream sauce, supple shrimp, jalapeño cornbread underneath. Share it. The rest of the menu is dense — pork du jour, shrimp and grits with local mushrooms, seared scallops, a Southwestern Caesar on grilled romaine.

Chef Andrew Araneo cooks hearty on purpose. This is not tasting-menu portioning. It is a Bayshore dinner that wants bread on the table.

**Plan it as** a filling night near the water when you want New Orleans energy without leaving the peninsula.
