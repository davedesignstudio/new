Sam’s Table is the opposite of a big-night warehouse. Chef Sam Stymest runs a tiny Bloomfield Avenue room on a monthly tasting menu — four courses, with optional bread and dessert — built around whatever the season is actually doing.

Winter menus lean on carrots, parsnips, cranberry, and juniper; spring brings onions, fennel, and peas. Guests are asked to pay attention to the fork, which is another way of saying this is not a share-plates hangout. It is BYOB, in the classic New Jersey small-room tradition.

**Plan it as** a tasting-menu date. Book ahead, bring a bottle that can travel from earthy to bright, and expect the menu you read last month to be gone.
