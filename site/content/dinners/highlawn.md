The Highlawn has always sold the view: a Florentine-style villa on a cliff in West Orange, looking at the New York skyline. Since 2024, chef Maxime Kien has been the reason to go for the plate as well as the panorama.

Open-flame meats and Prime beef share the menu with composed fish, including branzino with smoky eggplant caviar, lemon, and gremolata. It is still a celebratory-events building, which means the dinner can feel like a wedding even when it is not.

**Plan it as** a milestone night. Go before dusk if the skyline is half the reservation.
