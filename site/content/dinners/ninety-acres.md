Ninety Acres sits on the Natirar estate in Peapack: bucolic, modern, and supplied by the property’s own farm. The dining room was redone in 2024 when the adjacent Pendry Natirar hotel and spa opened, which makes dinner here feel like a short stay even if you drive home.

Seasonal plates do the estate math — pea-and-mint soup with crème fraîche and trout roe in spring, then duck, Prime steaks, and Berkshire pork with whatever the garden is growing. Opened in 2009, it is one of the Central Jersey rooms that never needed a Hudson County address to matter.

**Plan it as** a countryside evening. Pair it with a walk on the estate if daylight is still on your side.
