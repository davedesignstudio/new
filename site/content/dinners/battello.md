Battello is the Hudson dinner people describe when they mean a night that feels like an occasion. Chef Ryan DePersio’s waterfront room looks at the Manhattan skyline from Jersey City’s industrial-chic dining rooms and lounge, which is why it also books as a wedding venue — but the food is the reason to reserve on an ordinary Thursday.

Signature plates that keep showing up in 2026 roundups: pillowy ricotta gnocchi with sweet-sausage Bolognese, tuna tartare, and a spicy lobster risotto arrabbiata. This is not a red-sauce house. It is composed Italian cooking with a view, built for the table that wants both spectacle and technique.

**Plan it as** a waterfront date or a celebration. Ask for the skyline if the night is the point; stay for the gnocchi either way.
