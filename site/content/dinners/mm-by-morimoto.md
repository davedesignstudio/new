When MM by Morimoto opened in Montclair, it landed as a collaboration with the local team behind PastaRAMEN and Ani Ramen: an American chophouse crossed with modern Japanese cooking.

The orders that define the room are aburi sushi, a curated omakase, Japanese and Australian wagyu, and Morimoto’s black cod — the fish that followed him from his Nobu years. It is a lounge as much as a steakhouse, which makes it a North Jersey special-occasion default when you want sushi and a chop at the same table.

**Plan it as** a big night in Montclair when one cuisine is not enough.
