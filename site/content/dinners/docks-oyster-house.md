Dock’s is the Shore dinner with a date on the building: family-owned since 1897, set off the Boardwalk, still drawing a happy-hour line around the block. The raw bar is the opening argument. Mains run through crab, fried platters, a 16-ounce lobster tail, and whole lobsters from two to seven pounds. Steaks and rack of lamb keep carnivores in the room.

This is the Atlantic City dinner that predates the casinos and outlasted most of them. Energy in the dining room is part of the experience; quiet it is not.

**Plan it as** a seafood night on the island. Go early if you want happy hour; book if you want a table that is not a wait.
