Aarzu is the Central Jersey Indian restaurant that statewide critics keep returning to for both looks and heat. Duck paratha tacos and lamb rogan josh are the plates people quote; garlic naan is the standard that still lands a notch above expectation.

The room is celebrating a tenth anniversary with an interior refresh and new menu items — a useful excuse if you have been meaning to go and have not. It is a reminder that a New Jersey dinner map that only lists Italian and French is incomplete.

**Plan it as** a Freehold night when you want spice, polish, and a table that photographs well without eating like a photo.
