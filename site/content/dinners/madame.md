Madame is Jersey City’s argument that French bistro cooking can feel both romantic and current. Chef Jamie Knott — also the longtime force behind Saddle River Inn — treats classics as a playground: caviar on deviled eggs, focaccia served in a French-onion broth, black bass en papillote.

The Madame burger (Petit Basque, caramelized onions, truffle aioli, truffle frites) is the indulgent order. For dessert, chocolate pot de crème and profiteroles with banana gelato show up on every “don’t miss” list we found. The room reads speakeasy: dim, hip, and built for a late second cocktail.

**Plan it as** a date night when you want a full bar and French technique without leaving Hudson County.
