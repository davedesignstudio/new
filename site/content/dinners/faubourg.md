Faubourg (say *foh-boor*) is Montclair’s celebratory French room: a glass-enclosed patio, two bars, an upstairs lounge, and a private dining room from Dominique Paulin and chef Olivier Muller, both veterans of New York hospitality.

Start with tarte flambée (fromage blanc, smoky bacon) or gougères. The Weehawken location adds an open kitchen, a cocktail lounge, and dog-friendly outdoor seating in warm months — useful if the dinner is a Hudson walk plus a meal.

**Plan it as** a dressed-up night when you want a full bar and classic Gallic cooking in Essex or Hudson County.
