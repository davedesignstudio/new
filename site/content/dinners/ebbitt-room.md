The Ebbitt Room sits inside the Virginia Hotel, one of Cape May’s Victorian landmarks: white tablecloths, piano music, and a dining room that is friendlier and louder than the decor suggests. Regulars return every summer; first-timers get folded in.

The menu is tied to the restaurant group’s nearby farm, which supplies produce, meat, and dairy. That farm line is the difference between generic Shore fine dining and a Cape May night that still tastes like the county.

**Plan it as** a dressed-up Shore evening. Stay in the hotel if you can; walk the Victorian streets after dinner if you cannot.
