Judy & Harry’s is two rooms with one reservation: Judy’s for dinner, Harry’s for the nightcap. David Viana and Neilly Robinson — the James Beard-nominated pair behind Lita — treat it as Italian by way of a Jewish deli memory, which is a more interesting Shore story than another trattoria.

Sit at the chef’s counter if you can. The French-cut chicken parm is sized to share; cauliflower fried with pine nuts on lemon whipped ricotta is the side that people talk about after the parm. Then walk next door to Harry’s.

**Plan it as** an Asbury Park Saturday: dinner, lounge, ocean air.
