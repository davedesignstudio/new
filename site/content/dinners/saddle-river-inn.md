The Saddle River Inn is the special-occasion barn: a restored 1840 building where chef Jamie Knott has long cooked contemporary French food with Japanese inflections and a cellar that matches the room.

The lore is part of the dinner. A corner table is still pointed out as the one Richard Nixon used on alternating Thursdays when he lived nearby. You do not need the presidential footnote. You need the seasonal cooking and the sense that the night is supposed to last.

**Plan it as** an anniversary or a dress-up Bergen County evening. It is a destination, not a drop-in.
