June is South Jersey’s patient French classroom. Chef Rich Cusack and his wife, Christina, have spent five years teaching Collingswood the difference between a safe beef Wellington and a full plunge into escargots Bordelaise and sweetbread vol-au-vent.

Sundays help: a four-course tasting around $55, BYOB, which is one of the better introductions to French dining in the state. The chef still walks the room to translate menu French when people freeze. That hospitality is the point as much as the pastry.

**Plan it as** a Haddon Avenue BYO night. Sunday if you want the tasting; a weeknight if you already know you want sweetbreads.
