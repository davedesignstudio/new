## What is hidden

The ask.

This is the last one, and it is the only one you hid yourself. Nobody locked it away from you. You did it, early, because saying it plainly is worse than any storm:

**Am I allowed to stay?**

So you ask about the future instead. The future is a polite way of asking permission — it lets you find out whether there is a place for you without ever admitting you wanted one.

## Why a fortress hides it

Because the answer might be no, and a maybe can be carried indefinitely.

An unasked question has perfect odds. This is why people prefer prophecy to invitation. Prophecy costs a coin. An invitation costs exposure.

## How it surfaces

Somebody says *sit down* and your eyes sting for no defensible reason.

That is the whole tell. Not the grand moment — the ordinary hospitality that lands harder than it should, because it answered something you never said out loud.

## How she catches you

She stops selling.

For the first several cycles she deals honestly and lets you have your fortune. Then, once she has counted enough, she puts the deck down.

> “You thought you came for the future,” she says. “You did not. You came looking for someone to say you can stay.”
>
> “So. Stay.”

## Repair

Ask it. Badly is fine. Out loud, to one person, without dressing it as a joke or a plan or a question about next year.

Then set the table anyway, in case the answer takes a while to arrive.

---

The Tower falls, the Star rises, the same lightning tries the same wards tomorrow. Not a tragedy. A **schedule**.

The path that eats its own dust is still a path. Walk it kindly.
