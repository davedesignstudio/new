## The brief

A first-time owner opening a café in a building that used to be the town pharmacy. The goal: a brand that borrowed the building's history without turning into a theme.

## What we did

A name and wordmark drawn from the original storefront lettering, a compact colour palette, cup and bag graphics, and a one-page website with hours, the menu and directions.

## The result

A café that looked like part of the street from opening day, and packaging that people photograph, which turned out to be the marketing budget.
