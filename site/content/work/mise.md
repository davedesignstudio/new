The photograph is already a diagram. Bowls as common region. Tools as a second set. Black ground holding the field. The identity had to be as lucid as the mise.

**Similarity** turns the bowls into a system: same lip, same crop, same pause between units. Labels, takeout, and the menu inherit that rhythm so the brand feels prepped, not decorated.

**Prägnanz** cuts the mark down to the simplest stable form. No garnish on the logo. The food is the complexity. The brand is the board it sits on.
