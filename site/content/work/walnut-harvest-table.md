Designed and built for a farmhouse dining room that needed one piece to do all the talking. The top is book-matched walnut, eased at the edge, with breadboard ends so the grain can move. The trestle is through-tenoned and wedged — a joint you can see from the hall.

We drew the piece at full scale on the shop floor, then cut, fitted, and finished it in house. It is carpentry as furniture: heavy, quiet, and built to be used every night.
