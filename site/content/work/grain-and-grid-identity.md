We needed a brand that could stand in a shop and on a screen. The mark is a carpenter’s square locked to a baseline grid. Color comes from walnut, oak, cream paper, and copper. Type is a literary serif with a workhorse sans.

The stationery is cotton paper with copper foil. The brand book is bound in linen. It is identity as millwork: few parts, tight fit, made to be handled.
