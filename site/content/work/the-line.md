A kitchen already has a composition: the pass, the pendants, the stack of plates. The brand had to join that line, not paste a mark on top of it.

**Continuity** is the brief. The wordmark sits on the same axis as the heat lamps and the ticket rail. When a cook turns to the dining room, the identity is still on the path the eye is already taking.

**Figure and ground** does the rest. White jackets and white china advance. Steel recedes. The graphics stay in the white field so they can be read in motion, at dinner speed, without asking anyone to stop and decode.
