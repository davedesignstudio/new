A kitchen designed as furniture, not as a set of boxes. Tall oak cases run to the ceiling. Open shelves keep daily ware in reach. The island is a single thick maple top on a framed oak base, with room to sit and room to work.

We built the millwork in the shop, scribed it on site, and finished with a hardwax oil. The brass pulls will take a patina. That is the point.
