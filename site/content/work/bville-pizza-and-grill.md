## The brief

The Grill Family Restaurant had been feeding Bernardsville for years, but almost nobody called it that. Regulars said "B'ville" and left it at that. The owners wanted a brand that sounded like the place people actually talked about, and a menu and website that could keep up with a kitchen that changed its specials weekly.

## What we did

We started with the name. **B'ville Pizza & Grill** put the town first and the food second, which is exactly how the regulars already thought about it. From there we built a simple, sturdy identity that could survive pizza-box printing, a lit exterior sign and a phone screen at the same time.

The menu system came next: a dine-in menu, a takeout menu and a specials board that share the same grid, so an update in one place doesn't mean redrawing all three. The website put hours, the menu and online ordering above everything else, because that is what people came for.

## The result

A brand the neighbourhood recognised on day one, menus the owners could update themselves, and a website that did the one job it needed to do. Our first client, and the project that set the tone for everything the studio has done since.
