## The brief

Twenty years in, the food was better than ever and the menu was a laminated sheet from 2009. The family wanted something that matched the kitchen without losing the regulars who knew the old layout by heart.

## What we did

We kept the structure people knew and rebuilt the type, spacing and photography around it. A new backlit exterior sign and window graphics finished the job.

## The result

Higher check averages on the dishes the family is proudest of, and a sign you can read from the far side of the parking lot.
