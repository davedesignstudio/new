The Philhower & Okrogly site is a piece of the practice. It carries the identity, the work, and the theme song on a structure you can find your way around without a map.

Type, space, and photographs do the heavy lifting. The theme song player is the only flourish — and even that is a tool: press play, read the grain, hear the grid.
