The window was already the best seat in the house. We built it a frame: walnut benches with lift-up storage, open shelves to the ceiling, and a rail for a lamp.

The cushions are oatmeal linen over a firm foam. The oak floor continues under the seat so the piece feels grown, not dropped in.
