## The brief

A small bistro with a menu that changes every week needed a brand that could change with it without ever looking unfinished. The owners wanted the room, the menu and the sign out front to feel like one idea.

## What we did

A restrained wordmark, a warm palette pulled from the produce itself and a menu template the kitchen can fill in on a Monday morning. Interior graphics and a hand-painted exterior sign tied the room back to the paper on the tables.

## The result

A brand that looks considered whether the special is ramps in May or squash in October, and a menu update process that takes minutes instead of a design request.
