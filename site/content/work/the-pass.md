Sauce, plate, and the cook’s hand already know figure and ground. White field. Dark reduction. One motion that finishes the picture. The studio’s job was to get the identity out of the way and still be present.

The mark uses **closure**. It does not close its own shape. Guests complete it, the way they complete a plate that leaves a rim of porcelain open.

On the menu and at the pass, **figure and ground** stay strict. Type recedes. The dish advances. The brand is the table the work sits on — felt, not shouted.
