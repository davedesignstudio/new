Stairs are the most public joinery in a house. This one is white oak, with a stringer that shows its dovetails and a handrail carved to the grip of a hand, not a catalog profile.

The rise and run were set to the existing floors. Every tread is housed and glued, then pinned. Light from the skylight does the rest.
