## Seat

Klax navigates. Laminated itinerary, the careful one who has read every travel advisory and obeyed all of them until Bodie. Four-eyed only in the comic sense: he sees every risk and still gets in the van.

## Want / Need

- **Want:** get home before his moms learn he borrowed the van.
- **Need:** admit the detours — the auntie, the diner, the storm — are the life he actually wanted.

## Wound / Lie / Mask

- **Wound:** fear of disappointing the people who planned him.
- **Lie:** “If it isn’t laminated, it isn’t real.”
- **Mask:** longer balloon, caveats, “technically.”

## Arc (buddy B)

His plan burns early (good). He listens at the market when culture refuses the franchise rename. At the diner quarrel he is the one who almost ends the friendship to save the schedule. Reunion: he laughs once when the lever works without a list.

## Craft job

Straight man / observer who is also a full lead. Distinct need from Bodie (acceptance of mess vs admission of dependence). If both “learn to trust,” one of them is redundant — they do not.

## Voice

Lists. Caveats. Softens when the list fails. Never uses Bodie’s destiny words.
