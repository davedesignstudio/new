## Seat

Bodie drives. Grin, air-guitar, the wrong button at the wrong time. Failed community college twice and still believes the universe owes him a favor.

## Want / Need

- **Want:** the legendary Castle Nova slider — proof he is destined.
- **Need:** to say Klax’s plan was right out loud, without a joke on top.

## Wound / Lie / Mask

- **Wound:** being laughed at for trying; classrooms that felt like closed kitchens.
- **Lie:** “Confidence fixes everything.”
- **Mask:** destiny talk. Dude energy. Softens only when hungry or caught wrong.

## Arc (buddy A)

Shared exterior goal with Klax. Mid-journey breakup at the diner when confidence fails the basket. Reunion is not a speech — it is passing the ketchup. By the Star-table he can thank the itinerary without dying of embarrassment.

## Craft job

Wildcard half of the odd couple. He challenges Klax’s control; Klax challenges his denial. Chemistry is conflict under a shared hunger.

## Voice

Short sentences. Destiny words. One sincere line per episode maximum — make it land.
