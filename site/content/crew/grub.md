## Seat

Original snack-hound — Scooby energy without the licensed name. Slobber, food puns, under the booth. Panels love the nose and the empty bag.

## Want / Need

- **Want:** food; the Grub Gem; anything that smells like yes.
- **Need:** to be brave once without a treat first — to prove he is not only appetite.

## Wound / Lie / Mask

- **Wound:** treated as mascot / joke by prophets and scouts.
- **Lie:** “I am only appetite.”
- **Mask:** puns. Softens into silence when the mask comes off a real cook.

## Arc

He drives plot: rubber masks fall because he chews. Gary’s prophecy never named him; Zorya seats him anyway. His need beat is one panel of courage before the Gem — then the Gem feels like gratitude, not bribe.

## Craft job

Comic relief that fails the deletion test. Remove Grub and the unmasking stalls; the boys freeze; the franchise keeps the head. Humor comes from personality under hunger, not inserted gags.

## Voice

Food first. One brave silence per arc is louder than ten puns.
