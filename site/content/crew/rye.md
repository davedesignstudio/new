## Seat

**Rye** — Nina if you ask the nickel cafeteria, the extra place if you ask the road. Mustard worker coat. Winter scarf. Black loaf in one hand, coffee that does not spill when she bounces a fire escape. She is a guest, not a seventh main. Gary will try to announce her. Zorya deals her like anyone else.

She is not a god from the sky. She is the neighborhood after the splash: Tenth Avenue bleeding into a communal kitchen, a walking samovar on a clothesline, a black square that is a plate.

The first-issue page is [Sample](/sample).

## Want / Need

- **Want:** to keep the loaf moving — fire escape to fire escape, Automat to stolovaya, same supper.
- **Need:** to sit. Bounce is a job. The extra chair is the point.

## Wound / Lie / Mask

- **Wound:** kitchens that locked the crust and called themselves invincible.
- **Lie:** “If I keep moving I cannot be locked in.”
- **Mask:** kinetic grin, newsstand bounce, the coat that looks like a cape until you see it is workwear.

## Arc

She enters as splash: Hunger arrives as a body, the citadel thinks it cannot fall, she is already in the air with bread. She does not punch the storm. After the keep comes down she is the mismatched chair at the Star-table, passing rye. Gary starts a sentence about Chosen. She slides him the ketchup.

## Craft job

Guest echo of the extra place / [Keystone](/keystone). Street-level motion so the Tower-fall has a human scale. Foil to Gary’s grandeur and to any kitchen that armored itself. She must not solve the climax. She is arithmetic: one spare seat.

## Voice

Short. Concrete. One kitchen object per line. No destiny font. When she jokes, it is about nickels, crumbs, and gravity — never about ruling the night.
