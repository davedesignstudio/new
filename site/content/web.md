We brand the way we build: start with structure, choose honest materials, and refuse decoration that is not load-bearing.

A name, a mark, a type system, a website — they are joinery. If the joints are tight, the whole thing stands without shouting.

## Branding

- Naming and positioning for shops, studios, and places that make things
- Marks and wordmarks that work burned into wood and rendered in a browser
- Color, type, and a short set of rules you can actually keep
- Stationery, signage, and packaging when the brand has to live in a room

## Web design

- Sites for builders, makers, and hospitality — fast, readable, and easy to update
- Design systems that match the physical brand, not a leftover template
- Content structure first, then layout. Pretty that cannot be edited is a failed joint
- Launch, training, and the same aftercare we give a kitchen

## The dual brief

The best projects hire us for both. A millwork shop needs a site that looks like the shop. A new restaurant needs a room *and* a mark. We keep those in the same drawing set so the grain of the brand matches the grain of the build.
