## Carpentry design &amp; build

Custom work from drawing through install.

- Dining tables, benches, and casework in walnut, white oak, maple, and cherry
- Kitchens, libraries, window seats, and built-in millwork
- Stairs, railings, and exposed joinery
- Doors, paneling, and small structures
- Design only, build only, or design-build from first sketch

Every piece is drawn, cut, and finished in house. We prefer mechanical joinery you can see and oil finishes you can repair.
