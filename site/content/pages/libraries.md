We cannot knock on their doors. We can read what they left on the table: essays, interviews, craft books, the stories themselves. This page is that conversation, turned into decisions for **one** Earth plot. Methods, not plots. Nobody here is a Chosen One.

Who gets a seat. That is still the plot. Everything below is how other makers have said that, in other rooms.

The [kitchen](/kitchen) page is food. The [character bible](/crew/development) is seats. [Craft](/craft) is panels. This is the talk about **story**.

---

## Ursula K. Le Guin — the bag, not the spear

In *The Carrier Bag Theory of Fiction* (collected in *Dancing at the Edge of the World*), Le Guin argues that before the tool that forces energy outward, we made the tool that brings energy home. The Hero wants a story shaped like an arrow: start here, go there, THOK, the mark drops dead. She wants a novel shaped like a sack — a medicine bundle that holds things in relation. Conflict can live in the bag. Conflict is not the bag. Instead of heroes, people. The purpose is not victory or freeze-frame. It is **continuing process**.

**What we took.** Gary’s prophecy is the spear: Chosen Ones, a font that sounds like war, a slider as a trophy. The table, the plate, the van, the face-down card, the last bag of crisps — those are carrier bags. Sitting is the life story. Walking again is the sentence Le Guin ends on without ending: there are still seeds to gather. Earth does not THOK. Earth does not end.

**What we refused.** A how-to that says a story should be seen as a battle. Path B1 is a kitchen under weather, not a raid.

## Toni Morrison — retell, slightly edited

Morrison talked about storytelling as communal entertainment *and* education: the same stories told again, a little different, the room performing them together. You leave knowing something you did not know when you sat down. In *Beloved* she writes that it was not a story to pass on — which is itself a way of passing it on, under a hand.

**What we took.** Zorya’s loop is the same supper told again. Road-Wisdom and The Paperback are group performance: you choose, the room answers. The Hidden Parts are what an endless Earth **stores** instead of burying. We pass the story on. We do not pretend it is finished.

**What we refused.** A fortune that only happens once, to one person, in a language nobody else is allowed to speak.

## Scott McCloud — the unmask lives in the gutter

*Understanding Comics*: closure happens between panels. The reader co-authors what the ink refuses to draw. Time is a decision about gutters, not a caption that says *later*.

**What we took.** The ghost is a person. The unmask happens in the gap — rubber head off, clerk with a pan, auntie who was never dead. Silent diner panels. Road-Wisdom’s tiny LCD is a gutter: you imagine the kitchen the pixels cannot hold. Never say in a caption what the drawing (or the choice) already shows.

**What we refused.** Explaining the twist in the balloon that should have been steam.

---

## Vladimir Propp — a few functions, not thirty-one

*Morphology of the Folktale* maps skazka as functions: lack, departure, donor test, return, a wedding that closes the world. Useful as a lens. Fatal as a fill-in-the-blanks.

**What we took, lightly.** The lack is a seat. Departure is 11:40 and a van that starts on the third try. Zorya is the donor who **withholds** the magical agent — she leaves the card face-down so you will not spend the night obeying it. The “wedding” is sitting. The hound was not in the prophecy. The hound was in the booth.

**What we refused.** Running all thirty-one functions. WIDER is not a catalog of trials. It is six kitchens and a table.

## Interactive fiction — story first, then the fork

CYOA and IF craft (the old branch-and-bottleneck advice; delayed branching in later design talk) agrees on this: write the spine, then open doors that **reconverge** with state. Choices reveal values. Flags remember what you would not do. A first-choice walk should still reach an ending. None of the endings should say GAME OVER.

**What we took.** The Paperback: fifty-two numbered sections, nine endings, `need` / `fail`, Hidden Parts named when you refuse a seat. Road-Wisdom traits and kitchen flags are delayed branching — the Reader’s greeting changes because you passed the ketchup yesterday. You can walk again.

**What we refused.** A maze with no kitchen. A fail state that dumps you out of Earth.

## Abraham Lincoln — one small true story

Lincoln used anecdote as a safety valve and as illustration: a homely story could do what hours of argument could not. The point was not three jokes. The point was one picture the room could hold.

**What we took.** Zorya’s prairie tongue is **one** small true story, then she shuts up. If the leaves, the proverb, and the Tsar-winter skazka all speak at once, that is a sermon, not a teller.

**What we refused.** Stacking Lincoln / road / Tsar as three captions on the same panel.

---

## Hayao Miyazaki — steam first, then belonging

Ghibli kitchens linger. Cooking is not garnish; it is how a house admits you. In *Howl’s Moving Castle*, breakfast is the proof that the wreck can be a home. A person who cannot sit and eat is not yet in the family.

**What we took.** Write smell, then steam, then who gets a seat. VANESSA stalls when they hoard — the van is a house that will not move until the basket splits. Castle Nova is not a war. It is a counter, eight stools, a man who has been waiting up. He pours before he asks who you are.

**What we refused.** A legendary dish that exists only as a quest marker.

---

## Neil Gaiman and Jack Zipes — compost, not a museum

Gaiman’s working rule: we owe it to each other to tell stories. Myths are compost; you grow this year’s crop in last year’s leaves. Zipes on wonder tales: they stay alive by being “contaminated” — retold for the people in the room, not preserved under glass.

**What we took.** Tower and Star are tarot compost, not a licensed deck. Vasilisa on a high girder. A roast that remembers a Firebird without wearing the costume. Gary’s Chosen-One font is the fairy tale we contaminate until it becomes a receipt with a name on it.

**What we refused.** Pastiching someone else’s village. Zorya is a fictional traveling reader. Cards and hospitality are craft. They are not an ethnicity, and they are not a curse-for-coins act.

## Guest-friendship — feed first

Old host law (the Greek *xenia* of the roadside house, and every culture that salts a stranger before asking their name): you feed the guest **before** you ask who they are. The people who break that law are the monsters. The people who keep it are the story.

**What we took.** A closed fist cannot pass the salt. A house that will not feed a stranger is already a ruin. The diner waitress sets water before anyone has a name. The clerk with the pan is not a haunting; she is protecting a recipe. The villain is appetite that will not share. Heroism is seating fate. At the Star-table, nobody asks what you did to deserve it.

**What we refused.** A riddle at the door that you must win before you may eat. That is a fortress. We already know what happens to those.

---

## The storehouses — not every book, not every room

Nobody can list every book in every library on Earth, or find every library in all of time. The Mouseion at [Alexandria](/alexandria) is the one table we sat with until the crumbs spoke. [Stacks](/stacks) names other rooms — Nineveh, Nalanda, Fez, Dunhuang, Timbuktu — and admits the millions with no plaque. [Holdings](/holdings) is the union-catalog problem: a holding is not a title, WorldCat is not the planet, Sunday’s number is already late.

**What we took.** Pinakes as a table of persons and doubts. Store, don’t bury. Compare mouths.

**What we refused.** A fake complete catalog. Completeness as a spear.

---

## What we will not take from the shelf

- **Joseph Campbell as the model.** Departure / trial / return can describe a road. They must not crown a Chosen One. Gary already tried that. Zorya stole his lines.
- **Conflict as the only engine.** Le Guin already answered this. Hunger is weather. The bag still has to hold supper.
- **A story that cannot be told again.** Morrison and the Hidden Parts both refuse burial as the only ending.
- **Fortune as blood.** Zorya deals. She does not curse. She is not a cartoon of a people.
