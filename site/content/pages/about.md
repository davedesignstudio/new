## Our Story

Gable & Grain was founded on a simple belief: the finest homes are built one joint at a time.

Inspired by the Victorian craftsmen of the 1920s, we bring old-world carpentry techniques to the modern tiny house movement. Every gable, every moulding profile, every dovetail drawer is cut, fitted, and finished by hand in our workshop.

## The Craft

We work exclusively in solid hardwoods — quarter-sawn white oak, heart pine, and reclaimed chestnut salvaged from century-old barns. Our joinery follows traditional methods: mortise and tenon, dovetail, and pegged timber framing.

No particle board. No shortcuts. No compromises.

## Why Victorian?

The Victorian era produced some of the most ornate and enduring residential architecture in American history. We believe those details — the turned spindles, the fish-scale shingles, the bay windows — belong in homes of every size, including the smallest.

A tiny house should feel grand.
