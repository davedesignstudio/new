## A quiet ritual for the table

---

Soma Nord is designed around calm contrast: pale Nordic woods, Japanese
discipline, and Vedic-inspired warmth in saffron, turmeric, copper, and lotus
geometry. The kitchen keeps the plate direct and seasonal while the room gives
each course a sense of ceremony.

We serve with a simple principle: fewer gestures, deeper attention.
