This site is the pitch, the kitchen, and the **living skazka** for WIDER.

The teller is **Zorya of the Three Roads**: a fictional old Russian Roma roadside storyteller. She is written with respect for oral tradition — proverbs, cards, tea, crossroads — not as a cartoon. She does not “steal” or curse for coins. She **deals forks**, like the old Choose Your Own Adventure paperbacks.

The rest of the mash:

- space road-trip graphic novel
- Scooby-style unmaskings (original gang)
- Bill & Ted destiny + Harold & Kumar munchies
- Mad Libs names feeding her cards
- **SNES D&D riddles** — talking doors, rune order, ITEM commands, Hunger as HP
- Suno dashboard mix
- food-and-culture research as plot

Play her on `/play`. Each deal shuffles omens, so the same road is never told twice the same way.
