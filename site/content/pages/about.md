## Where three quiet traditions share one table

---

At **Lagom** we cook in the space between cultures. From Scandinavia we borrow *lagom* — not too little, not too much, just enough — and a love of clean lines, raw wood and unhurried light. From Japan we keep *ma*, the meaningful emptiness around each element, and the wabi-sabi beauty of the imperfect and seasonal. From the Vedic kitchen we draw *sattva*: gentle spice, warming ghee, turmeric and saffron chosen to nourish as much as to delight.

The result is a plate that feels calm and generous at once — Nordic restraint, Japanese precision, and the deep, grounding warmth of Ayurvedic cooking. We source close to home, waste little, and let each ingredient keep its own voice.

Sit. Breathe. Stay a while.
