WIDER is a playable comic told by **Zorya**, a fictional old roadside reader. She braids a short honest anecdote, traveling-kitchen proverb, and Tsar-winter skazka into one supper.

The plot is simple on purpose. A kitchen tries to be a fortress. Hunger knocks the walls down. The survivors eat. The van full of hungry fools was always driving to that table. The Game Boy road is you, walking the same Earth while she watches.

Food is the clue, not the garnish: who cooked, who was left out, who tried to franchise the bun.

Start with [the story](/story). Drive it in [The Paperback](/adventure). The six kitchens are on [the route](/route). What Earth stores instead of burying is in [The Hidden Parts](/hidden).
