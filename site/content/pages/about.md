## Three traditions, one table

---

Tasty Licks is a quiet room built on three ideas. From Sweden we take *lagom* — not too much, not too little, just enough. From Japan we borrow *wabi-sabi* and the reverence for empty space, for the single perfect ingredient left mostly alone. And from the Vedic kitchens of India we draw *ananda* — the warmth of saffron, cardamom and turmeric, and the belief that a shared meal is a small act of devotion.

The result is a menu that is spare on the plate and generous in spirit: cold raw-bar catch dressed in citrus and sea salt, plated beside gently spiced, slow-built warmth. Light wood, paper light and hairline lines. Nothing shouts. Everything is intentional.

Sit. Breathe. Eat well.
