## Built for the tunnel

Tunnel Test Pizzeria runs one long oven. Dough goes in. Pie comes out. If the crust doesn’t set, it doesn’t leave the belt.

We keep the menu short on purpose — margherita that exits clean, pepperoni that loops, specials that refuse the wrong toppings.

## For the love of food

Patterns and requests can describe a pie. Only a mouth can love one. We fire for the second case.

Come hungry. Leave with exit code 0.
