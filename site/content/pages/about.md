## Welcome to B'Ville Pizza & Grill

Located at **159 Morristown Rd** in Bernardsville, New Jersey, B'Ville Pizza & Grill served up a delicious blend of **Mediterranean and American cuisine** — from wood-fired pizza and gyros to ribeye steaks and fresh salads.

### Our Story

Originally known as The Grill Family Restaurant, B'Ville Pizza & Grill became a beloved neighborhood spot where families gathered for casual dinners, catering events, and celebrations. Owner Omar and the friendly staff were known for going above and beyond — from complimentary desserts to beautifully presented party platters.

### What We Served

- **Pizza** — Classic pies and slices, including their crowd-favorite cheese pizza
- **Mediterranean** — Chicken souvlaki platters, hummus, falafel wraps, and grape leaves
- **American Favorites** — Philly cheesesteaks, burgers, mozzarella sticks, and ribeye steaks
- **Salads & Sides** — Fresh house and Caesar salads, seasoned fries, and rice

Rated **3.8 stars** on [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/bville-pizza-and-grill-bernardsville) with 87 reviews, B'Ville was praised for generous portions, friendly service, and fair pricing.
