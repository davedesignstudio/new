## A meeting of place, practice and presence

---

Soma began with a simple question: what might happen if three food traditions met through shared values rather than novelty?

Our kitchen follows the Swedish seasons, practices the Japanese discipline of attention, and draws warmth and balance from Vedic food wisdom. We source close to home, preserve what the brief northern summer offers, and build each plate around clarity of flavour.

The dining room is calm, generous and informal. Come for six courses, a quiet celebration, or simply to taste the season as we see it.
