## Our Story

Slice Heaven started in a small kitchen with one wood-fired oven and a simple belief: great pizza doesn't need to be complicated.

Every pie is hand-tossed, topped with fresh ingredients, and baked at 900°F until the crust is perfectly charred and the cheese is bubbling.

## What Makes Us Different

- **48-hour fermented dough** for a light, airy crust
- **Locally sourced** produce and artisan cheeses
- **Made to order** — we don't slice until you order

Come visit us or order online for pickup or delivery!
