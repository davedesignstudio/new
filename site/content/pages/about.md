## A quiet table between three traditions

Rasa draws from Swedish modern calm, Japanese ma, and Vedic ideas of taste as feeling. The room is pale wood and ink shadow. The kitchen favors flame, fermentation, and spice that warms without shouting.

We cook for presence: fewer gestures, clearer flavors, and a meal that leaves space to breathe.
