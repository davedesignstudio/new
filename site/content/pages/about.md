## A quiet house for shared meals

Soma gathers Swedish stillness, Japanese precision, and Vedic hospitality into one table. We cook with the season, serve with unhurried care, and leave space — *ma* — between courses so conversation can bloom.

Our kitchen favors clear flavors: pine-smoked fish, rice polished to pearl, herbs warmed with saffron and sandalwood spice. Nothing loud. Nothing wasted. Every plate is an invitation to arrive fully.
