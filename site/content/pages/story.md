## What this is

WIDER is **one story about Earth**, told by a roadside fortune-teller named **Zorya**.

It is not a space war with a burger on the side. It is not a separate Game Boy toy, a separate Mars sim, and a separate restaurant brand. Those are rooms in the same house.

The house is a table.

---

## 1. You sit down

Zorya keeps a tent at the crossroads. One candle. One deck. She is an old woman of the road who learned three tongues and uses them as one:

- a short American anecdote (the Lincoln habit: answer a hard thing with a small true story),
- a proverb from traveling kitchens (fate is a guest; seat it),
- a Russian skazka from the Tsar’s winter (black bread, a stove that abandons a proud house, a Firebird that was always a roast).

She does not curse you. She **deals**. You choose. That is the whole mechanic, in clothes.

---

## 2. Hunger is the villain

The plot is not “save the galaxy.” The plot is **who gets a seat**.

A closed fist cannot pass the salt. A house that will not feed a stranger is already a ruin. Franchise scouts, phantom knights, talking mascots — they are the same guest: appetite that will not share.

---

## 3. The Tower was a kitchen

There is a citadel on a sea-cliff. Tarot calls it **The Tower**. Zorya calls it a kitchen that armored itself — recipes locked, wards up, pride in the beams.

You bind yourself to the hearth-stone. Then Hunger arrives as lightning. The glass breaks. Ghosts of people who ate without seating the village pour the courtyard. The keep cannot survive being honest. It falls.

From the dust: white stone, glass, a beam of light. The card turns. **The Tower becomes The Star.**

That is not a twist ending. It is the moral, visible:

**The fortress was never the point. The table was.**

Every night the Tower may fall. Every night dinner rebuilds the Star. That is the restaurant’s religion and the comic’s last panel.

---

## 4. The van was always coming here

**Bodie** drives. **Klax** navigates. **Grub** is the hound. **VANESSA** is the talking camper. They think they are on a stoner quest for one legendary **Castle Nova slider**.

They are guests.

Every “haunting” on their route is a person protecting food: a night clerk and the last bag of crisps; an auntie whose bun a scout wanted to rename; a diner that only starts the engine if they pass the ketchup; a mascot who is a franchise in a rubber head.

Unmask. Sit. The slider stand at the edge of the map is the Star-table with a bun on it.

**Prophet Gary** announced Chosen Ones. Zorya stole his lines. The hound was not in the prophecy. The hound was in the booth.

---

## 5. The little machine is still Earth

Road-Wisdom — the tiny Game Boy — is not another franchise. It is **you**, walking the same planet while she watches.

Forest, river, ash, snow, the old imperial mile. Cow Lick. Bville (Boom Boom or Chetzel, a plate unsplit). Cafe Robust. A yellow sun nailed to a post that says Philhower still builds.

She does not label your choices good or evil. She keeps a silent tally. After enough miles she turns five cards: where you came from, what follows, what you refuse, the road before you, what you become.

The cards do not predict the world. **They become the next stretch of it.** A bridge in the reading is a bridge in the morning.

A princess on a high girder is one old keep that would not come down — the Tower again, in a skazka dress. Climb, dodge, or ask her name. There is no GAME OVER. There is WALK AGAIN.

Mars is Earth’s dream of leaving. It still needs a table. If you can share a plate, the physics will forgive you.

---

## 6. What you were looking for

After enough cycles she stops selling you a future.

You thought you wanted a fortune.

You wanted **permission to sit**.

The path that eats its own dust is still a path. Walk it kindly. Earth does not end.
