## One plot

Hunger wants a seat it will not share. A kitchen puts up walls. The walls fall. What remains is a table. Earth continues.

## Three doors, same house

- **`/play/?b1=1`** — the storm, told straight: bind the hearth, stand the lightning, clear the courtyard, Tower becomes Star.
- **`/play`** — you name the guests (Bodie, Klax, Grub, the van) and walk. The “mystery” stops are kitchens. Unmask the ghost; it is a cook.
- **`/road`** — you walk a tiny Earth. Zorya reads your pattern. Cards you draw become the next mile.

Mars is not a sequel. It is Earth imagining an exit and still having to set supper.

Prairie anecdote. Road proverb. Tsar-winter skazka. Same stew, three spoons. She is a fictional traveling reader — not a cartoon, not a curse-for-coins act.
