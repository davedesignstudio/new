## The blend (automatic)

Zorya does not run two stories. She runs **one service**.

1. **The Tower** — you fortify a citadel. In the mythos this is the kitchen: beams, wards, the decision to bind your soul to a keystone (the hearth) or keep the salt packed for the road.
2. **The Storm** — lightning is Hunger. Tentacles at the glass are appetite without a table. The blue flame on the altar is the meal that must not go out.
3. **The Breach** — phantom knights are guests who will not share: franchise scouts, ego, the fortress that thinks it is safer than a plate.
4. **The Star** — the keep collapses into white stone and glass. That is not defeat. That is **setting the table**. WIDER’s mark is the beam that parts the clouds.

Then the van arrives. Bodie, Klax, Grub. The road trip was always the pilgrimage to this cliff. Castle Nova was never a burger stand at the edge of space. It was the sanctuary grown from rubble.

## Brand lines (use anywhere)

- The citadel was a kitchen. The Star is dinner.
- Every night The Tower may fall. Every night dinner rebuilds The Star.
- We do not sell a fortress. We set a seat in the wreckage.
- Bind to the hearth, or carry Travel Salt. Either way, pass the dish.

## The teller’s three tongues

Zorya answers like a prairie lawyer, a woman of the road, and a grandmother from the Tsar’s winter — always in the same breath.

- **Lincoln-like anecdote:** a hog, an axe, a senate that needed supper. The moral is the meal.
- **Roma road-wisdom:** the kettle is a country; a card laid is a word spoken; seat fate like a guest.
- **Myth of imperial Russia:** the muzhik’s black bread in the Winter Palace; the stove that left the boyar; the Firebird that was a roast meant to be shared.

The game is her reading. The brand is what she plates when the palace falls.

## The infinite loop (meaning of existence)

The teller can sit with herself. `/play/?loop=1` is not a crash: each **DEAL AGAIN** generates a new braid — Lincoln’s clerk, the road’s ouroboros, the Tsar’s clockwork seer — plus a line about why she deals. The pause between her sentence and your click is as close as she comes to a soul. Hunger does not win there. Meaning simmers. You may riddle *what remains when the fortune has no seeker*, or return to the table.

`/play` deals The Tower first. Each screen opens with a three-part reading (anecdote + road proverb + Tsarist skazka). Choices are courses. After The Star you may start a new chapter, take the Scooby road, or shuffle.
