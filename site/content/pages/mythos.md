## One plot

Hunger wants a seat it will not share. A kitchen puts up walls. The walls fall. What remains is a table. Earth continues.

## Brand lines

- The citadel was a kitchen. The Star is dinner.
- Every night The Tower may fall. Every night dinner rebuilds The Star.
- We do not sell a fortress. We set a seat in the wreckage.
- The path that eats its own dust is still a path. Walk it kindly.

## Her three tongues

Prairie anecdote. Road proverb. Tsar-winter skazka. Same stew, three spoons. She is a fictional traveling reader — not a cartoon, not a curse-for-coins act.
