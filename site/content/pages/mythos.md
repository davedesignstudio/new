## The blend (automatic)

Zorya does not run two stories. She runs **one service**.

1. **The Tower** — you fortify a citadel. In the mythos this is the kitchen: beams, wards, the decision to bind your soul to a keystone (the hearth) or keep the salt packed for the road.
2. **The Storm** — lightning is Hunger. Tentacles at the glass are appetite without a table. The blue flame on the altar is the meal that must not go out.
3. **The Breach** — phantom knights are guests who will not share: franchise scouts, ego, the fortress that thinks it is safer than a plate.
4. **The Star** — the keep collapses into white stone and glass. That is not defeat. That is **setting the table**. WIDER’s mark is the beam that parts the clouds.

Then the van arrives. Bodie, Klax, Grub. The road trip was always the pilgrimage to this cliff. Castle Nova was never a burger stand at the edge of space. It was the sanctuary grown from rubble.

## Brand lines (use anywhere)

- The citadel was a kitchen. The Star is dinner.
- Every night The Tower may fall. Every night dinner rebuilds The Star.
- We do not sell a fortress. We set a seat in the wreckage.
- Bind to the hearth, or carry Travel Salt. Either way, pass the dish.

## How to play it

`/play` deals The Tower first. Choices are courses. The blend into dinner is automatic — no menu interrupt until service, when you may start a new chapter, take the Scooby road, or change the teller’s voice.
