Zorya deals a card face-down and leaves it there a moment.

“Everyone asks what happens at the end,” she says. “Wrong question. Earth does not end. So nothing is gone. It is only **hidden** — and hidden things keep eating.”

Here is what a kitchen hides when it decides to be a fortress.

---

## 1. The names of the cooks

The menu survives. The recipe survives. The hand that learned it at four in the morning is scraped off.

That is the first hiding: **credit**. A scout wants the bun without the auntie. A franchise wants the flavor without the street. When you cannot name who cooked, you can pretend the food came from a logo.

**Where it surfaces:** the mask comes off and there is a person under it, tired, protecting a pan.

---

## 2. The guest list that was edited

Every table that closed did it quietly. No one announces exclusion; they just stop setting a place and call it capacity.

The hidden part is not hatred. It is the **arithmetic** — how many plates were ordered, and who was counted out before the doors opened.

**Where it surfaces:** an empty chair that somebody keeps wiping.

---

## 3. The reason the door was locked

The citadel says: for safety. Storms, thieves, competition.

Under that: a fear that if the recipe is shared, the sharer becomes ordinary. The Tower is not built against Hunger. It is built against **being replaceable**.

**Where it surfaces:** lightning. The keep cannot survive being honest, so honesty arrives as weather.

---

## 4. The debt nobody wrote down

Bind’s ledger is on the bar and it is not the real ledger.

The real one is unwritten: who fed you when you had nothing, who covered a shift, who left the light on at the mill. A house that will not admit its debts calls itself self-made.

**Where it surfaces:** a stranger who does you a kindness and does not mention it. That is a payment on an account you forgot you had.

---

## 5. The version of you that stayed

You keep walking. Fine. But every crossroads leaves a copy behind: the one who sat, the one who helped the child, the one who took the ferry when the smile was wrong.

The road hides them from you so you can keep moving. Zorya does not. She counts. That is what the tally is — **the hidden you, kept in her deck**.

**Where it surfaces:** five cards. Where you came from, what follows, what you refuse, the road ahead, what you become.

---

## 6. The exit that is not an exit

Mars. The next colony. The clean start. The wider dark.

Leaving is the most respectable hiding place there is, because it looks like courage. But the habitat still needs a table, and the same person who would not share bread here will not share air there.

**Where it surfaces:** a plate in the dust that is still warm. The physics forgive you only if you pass it.

---

## 7. The question under the question

You did not come for a fortune. You came to find out whether you are allowed to stay.

That is the last hidden thing, and you hid it yourself, because asking it out loud is worse than any storm. So you ask about the future instead, and the future is a polite way of asking permission.

**Where it surfaces:** after enough cycles, she stops selling futures and just says it.

---

## Why hidden and not gone

A story that ends can bury things. Earth does not end — so nothing is buried, only **stored**. Every closed kitchen becomes a rumor. Every uncounted guest becomes a ghost at the courtyard gate. Every unpaid debt becomes a mascot in a rubber head demanding your street.

That is the whole engine: **hunger that will not share does not disappear. It comes back wearing a costume.**

Unmask it. Sit it down. Feed it.

The Tower falls, the Star rises, the table is reset, and tomorrow the same lightning tries the same wards. Not a tragedy. A **schedule**.

The path that eats its own dust is still a path. Walk it kindly.
