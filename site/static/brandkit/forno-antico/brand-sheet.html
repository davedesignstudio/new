D. Philhower Design · Brand kit

Dal forno, con amore.

A wood-fired neighbourhood Italian restaurant. Family-owned, flour on the cuffs, the oven visible from every table. The brand should feel like it has been on this street for forty years — even on opening day.

1 · Logo suite

Three lockups. Never redraw, stretch, recolour or add effects.

  • Clear space = height of the flame on all sides. Minimum width: 120 px print / 32 px monogram.
  • On dark: use crema artwork (provided as knockout in print files). On light: ink artwork as shown.

2 · Colour

Crema leads, ink grounds, pomodoro punctuates. Oro is for special moments only.

Pomodoro#B33A2B · RGB 179 58 43CTAs, specials, seals
Forno#211D18 · RGB 33 29 24Type, boards, dark walls
Crema#F6F1E7 · RGB 246 241 231Backgrounds, paper
Olio#6B7042 · RGB 107 112 66Fresh/veg cues, subheads
Oro#C99A3F · RGB 201 154 63Rules, prices, foil

3 · Typography

Display · Fraunces (OFL)

Pappardelle al ragù della casa

Menus titles, signage, headlines. Sentence case, never all-caps. Italic for Italian words.

Text · DM Sans (OFL)

Hours, addresses, descriptions, web body. Regular 400, Medium 500. Letterspace caps (8%) for eyebrows like CUCINA ITALIANA.

Both fonts are open-licensed; web files ship with the kit (palette.css).

4 · Voice

Warm, short, sure of itself. Nonna, not a press release.

  • Say: “Wood-fired everything. Flour, tomatoes, patience.” Not: “An elevated artisanal dining experience.”
  • Italian words sparingly, always translatable at a glance: ragù (slow-braised beef & pork).
  • Sign-off line: Dal forno, con amore — from the oven, with love.

5 · In the room

How the pieces land, from the street to the table.

Menu excerpt

Pizza dal forno 14–19

Margherita classica — san marzano, fior di latte, basilico 14
Diavola — spicy salame, miele, smoked mozzarella 17
Funghi e salsiccia — roasted mushrooms, fennel sausage 18

Rule: dish name in Fraunces, description in DM Sans, price in Oro. Never more than 7 pizzas.

  • Street: ink fascia, crema letters, badge on the window. One A-board, chalk, changed daily.
  • Table: single-sheet menu (crema stock), badge-stamped box for takeout, oro-foil sticker sealing it.
  • Phone: monogram avatar, food-first photos, hours + menu link in bio. No filters heavier than the room itself.
